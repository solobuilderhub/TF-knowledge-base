# Sch 054: Low Rate Income Pool (LRIP) Calculation

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/054.json` and `extras/054.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this schedule to calculate the balance of the low rate income pool (LRIP) at any time in the tax year if you are a corporation resident in Canada that is:
  – a corporation other than a Canadian-controlled private corporation (CCPC) or a deposit insurance corporation (DIC)
  – a corporation that elected under subsection 89(11) not to be a CCPC
  – a corporation that was a substantive CCPC at any time in the previous tax year starting after April 6, 2022

• When an eligible dividend was paid or there was a change in the LRIP balance in the tax year, file this schedule with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return. Do not send your worksheets (pages 3 to 5) with your return, but keep them in case we ask to see them later.

• Unless otherwise stated, all legislative references are to the federal Income Tax Act.

• Subsection 89(1) defines the terms eligible dividend, excessive eligible dividend designation, general rate income pool, and low rate income pool.

• Subsection 129(4) defines the term aggregate investment income and subsection 248(1) defines the term substantive CCPC.

## Help text on individual lines

- **140**: Enter an amount on line 140 only if:
– you were not a CCPC in the previous tax year because of an election under subsection 89(11)
– you were a substantive CCPC at any time in the previous tax year starting after April 6, 2022
- **200**: Enter in column 1 each date where:
• an eligible dividend was paid in the year
• an adjustment was made as a result of an amalgamation or the wind-up of a subsidiary or on ceasing to be a CCPC (by an
  election or otherwise)
- **210**: Taxable dividends from a corporation resident in Canada (other than eligible dividends)
- **220**: Fill out Part 4 when the corporation ceases to be a CCPC or DIC. Fill out Part 5 for each predecessor involved in the amalgamation and Part 6 for each subsidiary involved in the wind-up. Add the adjustments calculated in parts 4 to 6 for the same date and enter the result in column 3 beside the appropriate date entered in column 1. Enter the total of column 3 on line 520 in Part 3.
- **240**: Includes taxable dividends (other than an eligible dividend, a capital gains dividend within the meaning assigned by subsection
130.1(4) or 131(1), or a dividend deductible under subsection 130.1(1))
- **510**: Include taxable dividends from a corporation resident in Canada (other than eligible dividends).
- **520**: Fill out Part 4 when the corporation ceases to be a CCPC or DIC. Fill out Part 5 for each predecessor involved in the amalgamation and Part 6 for each subsidiary involved in the wind-up. Add the adjustments calculated in parts 4 to 6 for the same date and enter the result in column 3 beside the appropriate date entered in column 1 of Part 2. Enter the total of column 3 on line 520.
- **540**: Include taxable dividends other than eligible dividends, capital gains dividends within the meaning assigned by subsection 130.1(4) or 131(1), or dividends deductible under subsection 130.1(1). For a non-CCPC with a tax year starting after April 6, 2022, include amounts paid in the previous tax year that did not reduce the LRIP or line 150 of the previous tax year, whichever is less.
- **amount 29 [a5_29]**: Calculate amount 29 for each predecessor.
- **amount 29 [a6_29]**: Calculate amount 29 for each subsidiary.
