                   Nova Scotia Financial Institutions Capital Tax                                        Schedule 353
                      Agreement Among Related Corporations                                                      Code 2101
                                 (2021 and later tax years)
                                                                                                           Protected B
                                                                                                         when completed

· Use this schedule if you are related financial institutions that are liable to pay the Nova Scotia financial institutions capital tax to allocate the basic capital

  deduction among the members of the related group.

  ­ $5 million, if the total amount of the capital of the financial institution and its related financial institutions is $10 million or less

  ­ $30 million, for a financial institution that is a trust company or a loan company with its registered office in Nova Scotia

  ­ the deduction is NIL in all other circumstances

· A related financial institution that has more than one tax year ending in a calendar year has to file an agreement for each tax year ending in that calendar

  year.

· A financial institution that is related to one or more other financial institutions at the end of a taxation year may file this agreement for the allocation of the

  basic capital deduction on behalf of the related group of which the financial institution is a member, for the taxation year. The amount allocated cannot
  be more than the basic capital deduction.

· Subsections 190.15(5), and (6) apply to this allocation if the references of capital deduction and corporation are read as basic capital deduction and

  financial institution respectively.

· According to subsection 190.15(5), when a corporation (the first corporation) has more than one tax year ending in the same calendar year and is

  related in two or more of those tax years to another corporation that has a tax year ending in that calendar year, the basic capital deduction of the first
  corporation for each such tax year at the end of which it is related to the other corporation is an amount equal to its basic capital deduction for the first
  such tax year.

· Section 190.15(6) provides for the limited circumstances when two corporations who would otherwise be related to one another are deemed

  not to be related.

· Unless otherwise stated, all legislative references are to the federal Income Tax Act.

· If you do not have enough space to list all the information, use an additional Schedule 353.

· File a completed copy of this schedule with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return.
