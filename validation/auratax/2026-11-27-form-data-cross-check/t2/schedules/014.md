# Sch 014: Miscellaneous Payments to Residents

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/014.json` and `extras/014.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• This schedule must be completed by all corporations who made the following payments to residents of Canada: royalties for
  which the corporation has not filed a T5 slip; research and development fees;management fees; technical assistance fees;
  and similar payments.

• Please enter the name and address of the recipient and the amount of the payment in the applicable column. If several payments
  of the same type (i.e., management fees) were made to the same person, enter the total amount paid. If similar types of
  payments have been made, but do not fit into any of the categories, enter these amounts in the column entitled
  "Similar payments".
