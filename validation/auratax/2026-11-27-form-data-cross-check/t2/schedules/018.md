# Sch 018: Federal and Provincial or Territorial Capital Gains Refund

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/018.json` and `extras/018.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this schedule if you are an investment corporation or a mutual fund corporation throughout a tax year to calculate the federal
  capital gains refund and the provincial and territorial capital gains refund (except for Quebec and Alberta)

• The terms investment corporation and taxed capital gains are defined in subsection 130(3); the term mutual fund corporation is
  defined in subsection 131(8); and the terms refundable capital gains tax on hand and capital gains redemptions are defined in subsection
  131(6) of the federal Income Tax Act

• The Ontario capital gains refund and Ontario refundable capital gains tax on hand are determined in subsections 106(2) and (3) respectively
  of the Taxation Act, 2007 (Ontario). The Manitoba capital gains refund and Manitoba refundable capital gains tax on hand are determined
  in subsections 9(1) and (2) respectively of the Income Tax Act (Manitoba)

• The Ontario basic rate of tax, referred to in Part 5 and Part 6 is calculated in Part 1 of Schedule 500, Ontario Corporation Tax Calculation.

## Help text on individual lines

- **144**: The amount of RCGTOH at the end of the year less the capital gains refund received for the year before amalgamation.
- **190**: (enter line 190 on line 788 of the T2 return)
- **amount 5A [a5_1]**: Enter the rate calculated in Part 1 of Schedule 500.
- **amount 5B [a5_2]**: The amount of ORCGTOH at the end of the year less the Ontario capital gains refund received for the year before amalgamation.
- **194**: The amount of ORCGTOH at the end of the year less the Ontario capital gains refund received for the year before amalgamation.
- **amount 6D [a6_4]**: Enter the rate calculated in Part 1 of Schedule 500.
- **amount 6E [a6_5]**: Enter amount 6E on line 260 in Part 11.
- **210**: For Newfoundland and Labrador, the capital gains refund as calculated above cannot exceed the least of:
  – Newfoundland and Labrador tax otherwise payable on line 209 on Schedule 5
  – the taxed capital gains on line 120 in Part 1 multiplied by the Newfoundland and Labrador income tax rate
  – Newfoundland and Labrador taxable income multiplied by the Newfoundland and Labrador income tax rate
