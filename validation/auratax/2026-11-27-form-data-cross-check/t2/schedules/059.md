# Sch 059: Non-qualified securities reporting

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/059.json` and `extras/059.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this schedule if you are an employer to report, as required under subsection 110(1.9), that a security to be issued or sold under an employee
  security option agreement between your employee and a qualifying person is a non-qualified security.

• A qualifying person is defined, in subsection 7(7), as a corporation or mutual fund trust.

• Unless otherwise stated, all legislative references are to the federal Income Tax Act.

• A specified person is defined, in subsection 110(0.1), as a qualifying person that meets all of the following:
  – it is not a Canadian-controlled private corporation
  – it has gross revenues (or total consolidated group revenue) exceeding $500 million generally for the last fiscal period of the qualifying
    person, for which financial statements (or consolidated financial statements) for that period had been presented to shareholders or unitholders,
    before the time the agreement is entered into

• The employee security option agreement is between a particular qualifying person and a taxpayer who is an employee of the particular qualifying
  person (or of a qualifying person that does not deal at arm's length with the particular qualifying person) to have securities of the particular
  qualifying person (or another qualifying person that does not deal at arm's length with the particular qualifying person) issued or sold
  to the taxpayer.

• Any of the following must be a specified person at the time the agreement is entered into:
  – the particular qualifying person that entered into the agreement with the taxpayer
  – the qualifying person whose securities will be sold or issued under the agreement
  – the qualifying person who is an employer of the taxpayer

• The employee security option agreement has to be entered into after June 2021.

• Securities are deemed to be non-qualified securities under subsection 110(1.31) or subsection 110(1.4).

• File this schedule on or before the filing-due date for the tax year of the particular qualifying person (which may be a person other than
  the employer) that includes the day on which the agreement is entered into.

• For more information on your payroll obligations relating to the exercise of these options, see canada.ca/taxes-security.

## Help text on individual lines

- **300**: Securities are deemed to be non-qualified securities in the proportion determined under subsection 110(1.31) if an annual vesting limit of $200,000
is exceeded. Securities are also deemed to be non-qualified securities under subsection 110(1.4) if they are designated as such by a qualifying
person that enters into a security option agreement with a taxpayer. Such a designation prohibits the qualifying person from filing an election
under subsection 110(1.1).
- **400**: Vesting year is defined under subsection 110(0.1). It is generally the first calendar year in which the employee may exercise the right
to acquire the security.
- **600**: At the time the agreement is entered into. The exercise price per security must be expressed as a decimal with a maximum of four decimal places.
