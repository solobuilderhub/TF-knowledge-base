# Sch 352: Nova Scotia Financial Institutions Capital Tax

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/352.json` and `extras/352.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• For use by corporations that are financial institutions with a permanent establishment (as defined in the federal Income Tax Regulations) at any time in the
  tax year in the province of Nova Scotia, including offshore areas, and that are liable to pay tax on their taxable capital employed in the province for the tax
  year, starting after October 31, 2021.

• Paragraph 69(1)(e) of the Nova Scotia Income Tax Act defines the term financial institution.

• To complete this schedule, you have to complete Schedule 38, Part VI Tax on Capital of Financial Institutions. File these two completed schedules with the
  T2 Corporation Income Tax Return within six months of the end of the tax year.

## Help text on individual lines

- **120**: A financial institution's capital deduction for a tax year equals its basic capital deduction (BCD), plus its investments in related financial institutions in the same year. The BCD is one of the following:

– $5 million, if the total amount of the capital of the financial institution and its related financial institutions is $10 million or less
– $30 million for a financial institution that is a trust company or a loan company with its registered office in the province
– For related corporations, the amount allocated on Schedule 353, Nova Scotia Financial Institutions Capital Tax – Agreement Among Related
  Corporations (see section 5 of the Nova Scotia Financial Capital Tax Regulations)
– "0" in all other circumstances
- **amount 1C [a1_3]**: Enter amount 1C on line 130.
- **amount 1F [a1_6]**: If line 129 of Schedule 5, Tax Calculation Supplementary – Corporations, is blank or "0", multiply by 1/2 instead of 1/3. If line 169 of Schedule 5 is blank
or "0", do not multiply by anything.
- **amount 1G [a1_7]**: Enter amount 1G on line 130.
