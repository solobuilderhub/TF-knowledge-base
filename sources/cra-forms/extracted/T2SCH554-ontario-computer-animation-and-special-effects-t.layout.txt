· Use this schedule to claim the Ontario computer animation and special effects (OCASE) tax credit under section 90 of the Taxation Act, 2007 (Ontario).

  Complete a separate Schedule 554 for each eligible production.

· The OCASE tax credit is a refundable tax credit that is equal to 18% of qualifying labour expenditures incurred after April 23, 2015, by a qualifying corporation

  in a tax year. These expenditures must be directly attributable to eligible computer animation and special effects activities (ECASEA) for an eligible production.

· For a film or television production for which the qualifying corporation commenced computer ECASEA after April 23, 2015, and before March 26, 2024, the

  production only qualifies as an eligible production if the qualifying corporation obtained a certificate of eligibility under subsection 91(15) of the Taxation Act,
  2007 (Ontario) for the Ontario Film and Television Tax Credit (OFTTC) or under subsection 92(8) for the Ontario Production Services Tax Credit (OPSTC).
  Complete Parts 4 and 5.

· For a film or television production for which a qualifying corporation incurred Ontario labour expenditures only on or after March 26, 2024, the production must

  incur more than $25,000 in Ontario labour expenditures in respect of the production to qualify as an eligible production. The minimum Ontario labour
  expenditure threshold must be incurred in the tax year of the claim, cumulatively between the tax year of the claim and the previous tax year, or in any
  previous two-year tax period. Complete Parts 6 and 7.

· To be eligible for the OCASE tax credit, the corporation must be a qualifying corporation and must meet the eligibility requirements in Part 3.
· Before claiming the OCASE tax credit, a qualifying corporation must obtain a certificate of eligibility from Ontario Creates, as required under subsection 90(7)

  of the Taxation Act, 2007 (Ontario). Only one certificate of eligibility is issued for all of the qualifying corporation's eligible productions for the tax year.
  Complete Part 2.

· To claim the OCASE tax credit, file this schedule and the certificate of eligibility with your corporation income tax return (T2 return) for the tax year.
