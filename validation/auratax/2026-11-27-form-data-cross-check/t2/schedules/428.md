# Sch 428: British Columbia Training Tax Credit

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/428.json` and `extras/428.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Complete this schedule if at any time in the tax year before January 1, 2028:
– you paid salary and wages to an employee enrolled in an eligible apprenticeship program administered through SkilledTradesBC
– your employee performed services related to the apprenticeship program at a permanent establishment in British Columbia

• You cannot claim the training tax credit if your principal business is the construction, repair or conversion of ships.

• To claim this credit, you must file a completed copy of this schedule with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return within 36 months after the end of the tax year in which you paid the eligible salary and wages.

• There are three elements to the training tax credit program:
1. Basic credit for an eligible recognized program (see Part 1).
2. Completion credit for an eligible training program (see Part 2).
3. Enhanced credit for eligible First Nations individuals and for persons with disabilities (see Part 3).

• Enter the identification number provided by SkilledTradesBC. If there is no identification number, enter the social insurance number (SIN) or the name of the employee. Also enter the name of the eligible Red Seal or non-Red Seal apprenticeship program and the salary and wages payable in the period. If you need more space, attach additional schedules.

• Do not complete Part 1 or Part 2 for an employee enrolled in an eligible apprenticeship program if you are claiming the enhanced tax credit for that employee in Part 3.

• Eligible apprenticeship programs and completion requirements are described in the Income Tax Act (British Columbia) and the Training Tax Credits Regulation.
