# Sch 034: Taxable Capital Employed in Canada − Financial Institutions

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/034.json` and `extras/034.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this schedule to determine if the total taxable capital employed in Canada of the corporation (a financial institution other than an insurance
  corporation) and its related corporations is greater than $10,000,000. Insurance corporations must complete schedule 35.

• If the total taxable capital employed in Canada of the financial institution and its related corporations is greater than $10,000,000, file a completed.
  Schedule 34 with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return no later than six months from the end of the tax year.  

• Unless otherwise noted, all legislative references are to the Income Tax Act and the Income Tax Regulations.

• Subsection 181(1) defines the terms financial institution, long-term debt, and reserves.

• Subsection 248(1) defines the terms authorized foreign bank and Canadian banking business.

• Subsection 181(3) provides the basis to determine the carrying value of a corporation's assets or any other amount under Part I.3 for its capital,
  investment allowance, taxable capital, or taxable capital employed in Canada, or for a partnership in which it has an interest.

• Regulation 8600 defines the terms Canadian assets and total assets.

## Help text on individual lines

- **[a2_L1]**: In the case of an authorized foreign bank, its eligible investments should be the amount before the application of risk weights 
that would be reported under OSFI risk-weighting guidelines. The investments should include only those used or held by the 
corporation in the year in the course of carrying on its Canadian banking business.
- **490**: In any other case, the investment allowance is nil.
