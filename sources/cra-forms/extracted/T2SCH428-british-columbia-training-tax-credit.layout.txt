· Complete this schedule if at any time in the tax year before January 1, 2028:
  ­ you paid salary and wages to an employee enrolled in an eligible apprenticeship program administered through SkilledTradesBC
  ­ your employee performed services related to the apprenticeship program at a permanent establishment in British Columbia

· You cannot claim the training tax credit if your principal business is the construction, repair or conversion of ships.

· To claim this credit, you must file a completed copy of this schedule with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return within 36 months after the end of the tax

  year in which you paid the eligible salary and wages.

· There are three elements to the training tax credit program:

   1. Basic credit for an eligible recognized program (see Part 1).
   2. Completion credit for an eligible training program (see Part 2).
   3. Enhanced credit for eligible First Nations individuals and for persons with disabilities (see Part 3).

· Enter the identification number provided by SkilledTradesBC. If there is no identification number, enter the social insurance number (SIN) or the name of the

  employee. Also enter the name of the eligible Red Seal or non-Red Seal apprenticeship program and the salary and wages payable in the period. If you need
  more space, attach additional schedules.

· Do not complete Part 1 or Part 2 for an employee enrolled in an eligible apprenticeship program if you are claiming the enhanced tax credit for that

  employee in Part 3.

· Eligible apprenticeship programs and completion requirements are described in the Income Tax Act (British Columbia) and the Training Tax Credits

  Regulation.

Part 1 ­ Basic tax credit

The basic tax credit is available during an employee's first 24 months of an eligible non-Red Seal apprenticeship program. To claim this credit, enter on
line 110 the salary and wages* payable during the tax year when the employee was still within the first 24 months of an eligible non-Red Seal
apprenticeship program. Do not include employees for whom you are claiming the enhanced tax credit in Part 3.

If your employee is in a Red Seal program, you cannot claim this basic tax credit.

   Part 3 ­ Enhanced tax credit

The enhanced tax credit is available if you have employees who are:

   · eligible First Nations individuals (defined as persons registered as an Indian under the Indian Act), or
   · persons with disabilities (defined as persons eligible, for themselves, for the federal disability amount on their income tax and benefit return)

Calculation for an employee's first 24 months of an eligible Red Seal apprenticeship program

To claim this credit, you must be eligible for the federal apprenticeship job creation tax credit (Red Seal programs only). Enter on line 411 the salary and
wages* payable that are eligible for the federal apprenticeship job creation tax credit (included on line 603 of
Schedule 31, Investment Tax Credit - Corporations).
