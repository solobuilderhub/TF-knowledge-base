# Sch 003: Dividends Received, Taxable Dividends Paid, and Part IV Tax Calculation

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/003.json` and `extras/003.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

**Takes values from:** Sch 043 [a4_1], Sch 043 line 360, Sch 043 [a4_3]

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Corporations must use this schedule to report:
  – non-taxable dividends under section 83
  – deductible dividends under subsection 138(6)
  – dividends deductible from taxable income under section 112, subsections 113(2) and 138(6), and paragraphs 113(1)(a), (a.1), (b), (c) or (d)
  – taxable dividends paid in the tax year that qualify for a dividend refund (see page 3)

• All legislative references are to the federal Income Tax Act.

• The calculations in this schedule apply only to private or subject corporations (as defined in subsection 186(3)).

• A payer corporation is connected with a recipient corporation at any time in a tax year, if at that time the recipient corporation meets either of the following conditions:
  – it controls the payer corporation, other than because of a right referred to in paragraph 251(5)(b)
  – it owns more than 10% of the issued share capital (with full voting rights), and shares that have a fair market value of more than 10% of the fair market value of all shares of the payer corporation

• If you need more space, continue on a separate schedule.
• File this schedule with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return.

## Help text on individual lines

- **240**: If taxable dividends are received, enter the amount in column G, but if the corporation is not subject to Part IV tax (such as a public corporation other than a subject corporation as defined in subsection 186(3)), enter "0" in column L (and column K, if applicable). Life insurers are not subject to Part IV tax on subsection 138(6) dividends.
- **amount = [a1_t1]**: (enter amount on line 402 of Schedule 1)
- **250**: Enter 0 if yes is selected in column H1. Do not include taxable dividends paid by the payer corporation, in its tax year in which it paid the dividend, that are suspended under subsection 129(1.3). For more information, see subparagraph 186(1)(b)(ii).
- **260**: Enter 0 if yes is selected in column H1. For eligible dividends received from connected corporations, Part IV tax on dividends is equal to column J divided by column I multiplied by column H. Do not include any taxable dividend deemed to be paid by the payer corporation under subsection 129(1.32) in the dividend refund calculation.
- **265**: Enter 0 if yes is selected in column H1. For taxable dividends received from connected corporations, Part IV tax on dividends is equal to column J divided by column I multiplied by column G
- **275**: For taxable dividends received from connected corporations, Part IV tax on dividends is equal to column J divided by column I multiplied by column G.
- **280**: For the purpose of calculating your eligible refundable dividend tax on hand (ERDTOH), Part IV tax on taxable dividends received from connected corporations (with a tax year starting after 2018) is equal to the sum of Part IV tax on eligible dividends and non-eligible dividends received from connected corporations to the extent that such dividends caused a dividend refund to those corporations from their ERDTOH.

Part IV tax before deductions on taxable dividends received from connected corporations for purposes of column L is the sum of (i) and (ii), where
(i) Part IV tax on eligible dividends received from connected corporations is equal to amount CC of the connected payer corporation (on page 7 of the T2 return) divided by line 465 of the connected payer corporation, multiplied by column G; and
(ii) Part IV tax on non-eligible dividends received from connected corporations is equal to amount II of the connected payer corporation (on page 7 of the T2 return) divided by line 470 of the connected payer corporation, multiplied by the difference between columns F and G.
- **100**: If yes, proceed to Part 3. If no, complete the rest of Part 2.
- **360**: (enter amount on line 712 of the T2 return)
- **[a23]**: Enter at amount AA on page 7 of the T2 return
- **[a19]**: (enter at amount C on page 7 of the T2 return)
- **[a22]**: (enter at amount D on page 7 of the T2 return)
- **[a24]**: Enter at amount DD on page 7 of the T2 return
