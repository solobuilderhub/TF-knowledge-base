# Sch 067: Canada Recovery Dividend

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/067.json` and `extras/067.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this schedule to calculate the Canada recovery dividend under Part VI.2 if you were a bank or life insurer group member at any time during a 2021 tax year.

• The Canada recovery dividend is a temporary additional tax on the taxable income of certain bank or life insurer group members. It is imposed for the 2022 tax year and payable in equal instalments over five successive tax years (beginning with the 2022 tax year).

• If you have more than one 2022 tax year, the latest 2022 tax year is used for the purposes of calculating the Part VI.2 tax payable.

• A bank or life insurer group member is a bank, a life insurance corporation that carries on business in Canada, or any financial institution (as defined in subsection 190(1)) that is related to a bank or life insurance corporation that carries on business in Canada.

• Where there has been an amalgamation of two or more corporations, the amalgamated corporation is treated as being the same corporation as its predecessor corporations for the purposes of calculating Part VI.2 tax.

• Where a subsidiary has been wound up into its parent corporation, the parent corporation is deemed to be a continuation of its subsidiary for the purposes of calculating Part VI.2 tax.

• Unless otherwise stated, all legislative references are to the federal Income Tax Act.

• If you are liable to pay tax under Part VI.2 for the 2022 tax year, file a completed copy of this schedule with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return.

## Help text on individual lines

- **105**: (If the corporation is not registered, enter "NR")
- **amount = [a1_table_total]**: (not to exceed 1 billion dollars)
- **120**: If you didn't file an agreement under subsection 191.5(5) within 30 days after receiving the Minister's request, enter the lesser of the following two amounts:
- the amount allocated to your corporation in column 3
- the amount allocated by the Minister under subsection 191.5(6)

If you didn't file the agreement and the Minister made no allocation for the group members, enter "0".
- **200**: The amount of taxable income (or the amount of the taxable income earned in Canada, if you are non-resident in the tax year), is determined in accordance with Part I, without regard to paragraphs 111(1)(a) and (b). If there was an amalgation or wind-up in 2020 or 2021, include the taxable income of the predecessor corporations or subsidiaries.

If you have multiple 2020 or 2021 tax years, and the total number of days in all your 2020 or 2021 tax years is greater than 365 days, your taxable income determined for the 2020 or 2021 tax year shall be reduced to the proportion that 365 is of the total number of days in all your 2020 or 2021 tax years.
- **210**: The amount of taxable income (or the amount of the taxable income earned in Canada, if you are non-resident in the tax year), is determined in accordance with Part I, without regard to paragraphs 111(1)(a) and (b). If there was an amalgation or wind-up in 2020 or 2021, include the taxable income of the predecessor corporations or subsidiaries.

If you have multiple 2020 or 2021 tax years, and the total number of days in all your 2020 or 2021 tax years is greater than 365 days, your taxable income determined for the 2020 or 2021 tax year shall be reduced to the proportion that 365 is of the total number of days in all your 2020 or 2021 tax years.
- **220**: Your income deduction for the 2022 tax year, under subsection 191.5(2), is equal to one of the following two amounts, whichever is applicable:
- $1 billion, if you are not related to another bank or life insurer group member at the end of the 2021 tax year
- amount from line 120, if you are related to another bank or life insurer group member at the end of the 2021 tax year
- **amount C [a2_C]**: If amount C is negative, enter "0"
