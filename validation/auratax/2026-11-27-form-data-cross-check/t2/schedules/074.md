## Help text on individual lines

- **125**: Assign a number to each designated work site and keep a record in case the Canada Revenue Agency asks to see it later. If there is more than one designated work site associated with the property, you must elect to meet the labour requirements for each designated work site to claim at the regular tax credit rate.
- **130**: Exclude any part of the capital cost of the eligible clean hydrogen property that is unpaid on the 180th day after the end of the tax year in which the property became available for use. Include this unpaid amount in the capital cost at the time it is paid. For more information, see subsection 127.48(13).
- **135**: Include government and non-government assistance that you received, that you are entitled to receive, or that you can reasonably expect to receive in respect of the property. For information about other types of adjustments to include in this column, see subsection 127.48(10).
- **140**: Include government and non-government assistance that you repaid (or that you have not received and can no longer reasonably expect to receive) that was applied to reduce the capital cost of a property for a previous tax year. For more information about the repayment of assistance, see subsection 127.48(11).
- **150**: If the property is dual-use electricity and heat equipment, project support equipment, or certain supporting equipment that is not used all or substantially all (ASA) to support the qualified clean hydrogen project, enter the percentage for the proportion of the capital cost to be included. For any other property, or the described equipment that is used ASA to support the project, enter 100%. For more information, see paragraph 127.48(10)(f).
- **160**: If the property is dual-use hydrogen and ammonia equipment, dual-use electricity and heat equipment, project support equipment, or certain supporting equipment and is used in the production of hydrogen and ammonia, enter the percentage of the expected use of the equipment that is attributable to hydrogen production. For any property that is used only in hydrogen production, enter 100%.
Otherwise, enter 0%. For more information, see paragraph 127.48(10)(g).
- **165**: If the property is dual-use hydrogen and ammonia equipment, dual-use electricity and heat equipment, project support equipment, or certain supporting equipment and is used in the production of hydrogen and ammonia, enter the percentage of the expected use of the equipment that is attributable to ammonia production. For any property that is used only in ammonia production, enter 100%.
Otherwise, enter 0%. For more information, see paragraph 127.48(10)(g).
- **195**: Enter the amount from line 195 on line 140 of Schedule 31, Investment Tax Credit – Corporations.
- **310**: For each covered worker at each designated work site, calculate the number of days they were paid below the prevailing wage requirements. Add these amounts together and enter the result on line 310.
- **315**: For any part of a tax year after December 31, 2023, adjust this amount for inflation using the consumer price index and prorate it accordingly (see subsection 127.46(8)). For more information, go to canada.ca/clean-economy-credits-employer-compliance.
- **[a3_subtitle3]**: The maximum percentage of apprentices to journeypersons is based on the applicable law or collective agreement. For more information, see paragraph 127.46(5)(b).
- **330**: For any part of a tax year after December 31, 2023, adjust this amount for inflation using the consumer price index and prorate it accordingly. For more information, see canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/corporations/business-tax-credits/clean-economy-itc/labourrequirements-itc.html#compliance.
- **340**: Enter the amount from line 340 on line 580 of the T2 return.
- **625**: If the property is disposed of to a person or partnership who deals at arm's length with the corporation, enter the proceeds of disposition. In any other case, enter the fair market value. The amount in this column cannot be greater than the amount in column 6G.
- **650**: Enter the amount from line 650 on Schedule 31, Investment Tax Credit – Corporations.
- **720**: If you responded yes on line 715 and paragraph 127.48(8)(b) applies, the deemed average actual carbon intensity is equal to the expected carbon intensity set out in the revised plan.
If you responded yes on line 715 and paragraph 127.48(8)(c) applies, enter 4.6.
If you responded no on line 715 and paragraph 127.48(8)(d) applies, enter 4.6.
- **750**: Enter the specified percentage that would have applied to the capital cost of the property according to the average actual carbon intensity on line 710, or, if the compliance period has not started, enter the specified percentage according to the deemed average actual carbon intensity on line 720.
- **765**: Enter the amount from line 765 on Schedule 31, Investment Tax Credit – Corporations.
