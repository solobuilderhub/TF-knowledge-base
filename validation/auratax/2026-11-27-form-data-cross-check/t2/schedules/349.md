# Sch 349: Nova Scotia Innovation Equity Tax Credit

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/349.json` and `extras/349.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• You can claim a 15% non-refundable Nova Scotia innovation equity tax credit under section 37A of the Nova Scotia Income Tax Act and the Innovation Equity Tax Credit Regulations, if:
  – you are an eligible investor that is a corporation
  – you have received a tax credit certificate from the Minister of Finance and Treasury Board of Nova Scotia

• The credit applies to eligible investments made before March 1, 2029. The credit you earned in the year is used to reduce your Nova Scotia income tax payable for that year. Any unused credit can be carried forward for seven years or carried back to the three previous tax years.

• The maximum amount you can claim for a particular tax year is the lesser of:
  – the aggregate of the innovation equity tax credit for the year and any eligible amount that has been carried forward or carried back – $75,000

• Attach this schedule and a copy of your tax credit certificate(s) to your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return.
