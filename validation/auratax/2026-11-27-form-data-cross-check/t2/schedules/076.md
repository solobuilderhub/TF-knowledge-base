# Sch 076: Clean Technology Manufacturing Investment Tax Credit

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/076.json` and `extras/076.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this schedule to calculate your clean technology manufacturing (CTM) investment tax credit (ITC) if you are a taxable Canadian corporation or a taxable Canadian corporation that is a member of a partnership that has acquired CTM property after December 31, 2023, and before January 1, 2035, that became available for use during that period.

• Use this schedule to report any recapture of CTM ITC. For more information, see subsections 127.49(11) to (15).

• The CTM ITC is a specified percentage of the capital cost of the corporation's CTM property acquired in the year for a CTM use, plus any CTM ITC allocated from partnerships under subsection 127.49(8). A CTM property is deemed not to have been acquired by the corporation until it has become available for use.

• The specified percentage is based on when the CTM property was acquired and became available for use:
  – 30% after December 31, 2023, and before January 1, 2032
  – 20% after December 31, 2031, and before January 1, 2033
  – 10% after December 31, 2032, and before January 1, 2034
  – 5% after December 31, 2033, and before January 1, 2035
  – nil after December 31, 2034

• You cannot claim the CTM ITC for an amount for which a CTM ITC was previously deducted by any person, or for which any other clean economy tax credit (as defined in subsection 127.47(1)) is deducted by any person. You cannot claim the CTM ITC for eligible property if a carbon capture utilization and storage (CCUS) ITC or clean hydrogen ITC is claimed for that same property. You may claim the CTM ITC and the ITCs in section 127 (for example, the Atlantic ITC) for the same eligible property.

• The terms CTM property, CTM use, government assistance, independent engineer or geoscientist, non-CTM use, non-government assistance, qualifying material, qualifying mineral activity, safe harbour price method, specified fair market value method, and specified percentage are defined in subsection 127.49(1).

• Unless otherwise stated, all legislative references are to the Income Tax Act.

• File this schedule with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return on or before the later of December 31, 2026, and one year after your filing-due date for the tax year in which the CTM property became available for use. If a recapture of the ITC occurs in the tax year (see Part 2), you must file this schedule no later than your filing-due date for the tax year.

• For more information, go to canada.ca/clean-economy-credits.

## Help text on individual lines

- **106**: For each CTM property, enter the asset code corresponding to its CTM use. See the asset code descriptions on page 3.
If you entered code 30 in column 1C, the CTM ITC in respect of that CTM property is nil unless you attach to this schedule a certification by an independent engineer or geoscientist. For more information, see subsection 127.49(2.1).

If you entered code 30 or 31 in column 1C, you need to elect in respect of that CTM property that the method for determining the value of all commercial outputs from that property is based on either the specified fair market value method or the safe harbour price method. The election must be filed together with this schedule and the method elected for the property is binding for all relevant tax years. For more information, see subsection 127.49(2.2).
- **110**: The available-for-use date must be within the current tax year. The property cannot be used, or acquired for use or lease, for any purpose before it was acquired. For more information, see subsection 127.49(4).
- **115**: If any part of the capital cost of the CTM property is unpaid on the 180th day after the end of the tax year in which the property became available for use, that part is excluded from the capital cost of such property in the year and can only be added to the capital cost of a separate CTM property that is deemed to be acquired at the time it is paid. For more information, see subsection 127.49(9)
- **120**: Include government and non-government assistance you received, are entitled to receive, or can reasonably expect to receive for the property. For information about other types of adjustments to be included in this column, see subsection 127.49(5).
- **125**: Include government and non-government assistance that you repaid, or you have not received and can no longer reasonably expect to receive, that was applied to reduce the capital cost of a CTM property for a previous tax year. For more information about the repayment of assistance, see subsection 127.49(7).
- **amount A [a1_lA]**: total of column 1K
- **220**: If the CTM property is disposed of to a person or partnership who deals at arm's length with the corporation, enter the proceeds of disposition. If the CTM property is disposed of to a person or partnership who does not deal at arm's length with the corporation, is converted to a non-CTM use, or is exported from Canada, enter the fair market value. See subsection 127.49(12) for more information.
- **amount B [a2_lB]**: total of column 2I
