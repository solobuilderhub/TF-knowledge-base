# Sch 065: Air Quality Improvement Tax Credit

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/065.json` and `extras/065.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this schedule if you are a qualifying corporation to claim the air quality improvement tax credit under section 127.43 of the federal Income Tax Act.

• The air quality improvement tax credit is a refundable tax credit available to eligible entities for qualifying expenditures made for air quality 
  improvements in qualifying locations during the qualifying period.

• Eligible entities means qualifying corporations, individuals (other than trusts) and partnerships.

• The qualifying period starts on September 1, 2021, and ends on December 31, 2022.

• To claim the air quality improvement tax credit, file a completed copy of this schedule with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return.

• The terms: assistance, eligible entity, qualifying corporation, qualifying expenditure, qualifying location, qualifying period, total per location
  expense, and total ventilation expense, are defined in subsection 127.43(1).

• Unless otherwise stated, all legislative references are to the federal Income Tax Act.

## Help text on individual lines

- **120**: For an associated corporation, the taxable capital employed in Canada is as of its last tax year that ended before the start of your current tax year.
- **210**: See subsection 9700(2) of the Income Tax Regulations for the list of outlays and expenses prescribed.
For your first tax year ending in 2022, include amounts of qualifying expenditures made or incurred from September 1, 2021.
If your tax year ends after 2022, enter the amounts made or incurred in the current tax year until December 31, 2022.
- **220**: Include all amounts of assistance you received, are entitled to receive or can reasonably be expected to receive for qualifying 
expenditures included in column 2, which have not been repaid before the end of the tax year under a legal obligation.
- **240**: If two eligible entities are affiliated with the same eligible entity, they are deemed to be affiliated with each other.
- **310**: The identification number is the social insurance number, business number or partnership account number of the eligible entity.
- **400**: Enter 100% or the percentage allocated to the corporation in Part 3.
- **500**: If a corporation is a member of a partnership at the end of a fiscal period of the partnership that ends after 2021 and that ends in a tax year of the
corporation, the air quality improvement tax credit allocated from the partnership is equal to the corporation's specified proportion of the air quality
improvement tax credit of the partnership for the fiscal period.
Enter the total of all air quality improvement tax credit provided by the partnerships of which you are a member.
