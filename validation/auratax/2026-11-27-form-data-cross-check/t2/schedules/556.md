# Sch 556: Ontario Film and Television Tax Credit (OFTTC)

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/556.json` and `extras/556.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this schedule to claim an Ontario film and television tax credit (OFTTC) under section 91 of the Taxation Act, 2007 (Ontario). Complete a separate Schedule 556 for each eligible Ontario production.

• The OFTTC is a refundable tax credit based upon qualifying labour expenditures incurred by a qualifying production company in a tax year for eligible Ontario productions.

• Before claiming an OFTTC, a qualifying production company must apply online to Ontario Creates for a certificate of eligibility. If the production is eligible, Ontario Creates will issue a certificate certifying that the production is an eligible Ontario production and certifying the estimated amount of the corporation's OFTTC for the production. Enter the certificate information and identification for this production in Part 2 of this schedule.

• If you claim, or have claimed, the Ontario production services tax credit for a production for any tax year, the OFTTC you are entitled to is nil for that same eligible Ontario production.

• To claim the OFTTC, you must meet the eligibility requirements in Part 3 of this schedule.

• To claim the OFTTC, include a completed copy of this schedule for each production with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return for the tax year as well as a copy of the certificate of eligibility issued by Ontario Creates.

- **550**: Include only the following Ontario refundable tax credits and assistance:
 Ontario Book Publishing Tax Credit
– Ontario Computer Animation and Special Effects Tax Credit
– Ontario Sound Recording Tax Credit
– Ontario Film and Television Tax Credit
– Payments from the 2015 Ontario Production Services and Computer Animation and Special Effects Transitional Fund administered by Ontario Creates
- **555**: 2 Include only the following Federal tax credits: Film or Video Production Services Tax Credit, Investment tax credit, and Investment tax credit of cooperative corporation.
- **805**: If the small first-time production is a regional Ontario production, enter $20,000 on line 805; otherwise, enter $15,000.
