Without it, a negative successor amount available of Area E is deducted at line 167, and the negative regular amount available of Area E that this causes at line 133 in Area D. With it, a negative successor amount available of Area E is deducted at line 133, and a negative successor amount available of Area D at line 167. Any other negative regular amount available of Area E is deducted at line 105.
- **247**: Foreign exploration and development expenses transferred on amalgamation or wind-up to which federal subsections 87(1.2) and 88(1.5) apply should be entered in column C if the expenses were regular expenses in the hands of the amalgamating company or the subsidiary being wound up.

     a) the total of all amounts available in column E; and
