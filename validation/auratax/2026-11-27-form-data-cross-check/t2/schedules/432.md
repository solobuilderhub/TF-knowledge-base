# Sch 432: Additional Certificate Numbers for the British Columbia Clean Buildings Tax Credit

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/432.json` and `extras/432.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this schedule if you have more than one certificate number for the British Columbia clean buildings tax credit that you can claim for the tax year.

• Claim the total British Columbia clean buildings tax credit on Schedule 5, Tax Calculation Supplementary – Corporations, and file it with the T2 Corporation Income Tax Return together with this schedule. You do not have to file the certificates of eligibility with your return, but you must keep them in case the Canada Revenue Agency asks for them later.
