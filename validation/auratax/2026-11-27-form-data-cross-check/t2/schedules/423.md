# Sch 423: British Columbia Production Services Tax Credit

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/423.json` and `extras/423.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this form to claim the following credits under the Income Tax Act (British Columbia):
  – production services tax credit (section 82.1), complete Parts 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, and 11
  – regional production services tax credit (section 82.2), complete Part 7
  – distant location production services tax credit (section 82.21), complete Part 8
  – digital animation, visual effects and post-production services tax credit (section 82.3), complete Parts 5 and 9
  – major production tax credit (section 82.4), complete Part 10

• To claim any of the above credits, include the following with the T2 Corporation Income Tax Return:
  – accreditation certificate (or a copy)
  – if claiming the major production tax credit, include a copy of the major production certificate.
  – a completed copy of this form for each accredited production. We consider each episode in a series to be a production. However, we will accept one form for episodes in a series that are accredited productions.

• Claim these credits no later than 18 months after the end of the tax year.
