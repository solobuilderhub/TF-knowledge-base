# Sch 075: Clean Technology Investment Tax Credit

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/075.json` and `extras/075.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this schedule to calculate your clean technology investment tax credit (ITC) if you are a taxable Canadian corporation or a taxable Canadian corporation that is a member of a partnership, that acquired clean technology property after March 27, 2023, and before January 1, 2035, that became available for use during that period.

• The clean technology ITC is a specified percentage of the capital cost of the corporation's clean technology property based on when it was acquired and
became available for use:
  – 30% after March 27, 2023, and before 2034
  – 15% in 2034
  – nil after 2034

• You cannot claim the clean technology ITC for an amount for which a clean technology ITC was previously deducted by any person, or for which any other clean economy tax credit (as defined in subsection 127.47(1)) is deducted by any person. You cannot claim the clean technology ITC for eligible property if a carbon capture, utilization, and storage ITC or clean hydrogen ITC is claimed for that same property. You may claim the clean technology ITC and the ITCs in section 127 (for example, the Atlantic ITC) for the same eligible property.

• You may claim the clean technology ITC only if you substantially comply with the requirements of any environmental laws, by-laws, and regulations applicable to the otherwise eligible property (see subsection 127.45(5.1)).

• Labour requirements apply to clean technology property prepared or installed after November 27, 2023. If you do not elect to meet the labour requirements in Part 3, you can only claim the clean technology ITC at the reduced tax credit rate, which equals the regular tax credit rate (specified percentage) minus 10 percentage points. The labour requirements do not apply to the preparation or installation of clean technology property that is described in subparagraph (d)(i) of class 43.1 or in class 56 in Schedule II of the Income Tax Regulations.

• The terms clean technology property, government assistance, non-clean technology use, non-government assistance, and specified percentage are defined in subsection 127.45(1).

• The terms apprenticeship requirements, covered worker, designated work site, installation taxation year, prevailing wage requirements, Red Seal trade, Red Seal worker, and regular tax credit rate are defined in subsection 127.46(1).

• Unless otherwise stated, all legislative references are to the Income Tax Act.

• If you have received a technical opinion from Natural Resources Canada on any of the property for which you are claiming this ITC, you may attach a copy of it to this schedule.

• File this schedule with your corporation income tax return (T2 return) on or before the later of December 31, 2026, and one year after your filing due date for the tax year in which the property became available for use. In the case of a recapture, you must file this schedule no later than your filing due date for the tax year in which the recapture event occurs (see Part 2).

• For more information, go to canada.ca/clean-economy-credits.

• If you need more space, attach additional sheets.

## Help text on individual lines

- **105**: If the property is a refurbishment, note this in the description.
- **122**: Assign a number to each designated work site and keep a record in case the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) asks to see it later. If there is more than one designated work site associated with the property and you elect to meet the labour requirements, you must meet them at each of your designated work sites to claim at the regular tax credit rate.
- **125**: Exclude any part of the capital cost of the clean technology property that is unpaid on the 180th day after the end of the tax year in which the deduction in respect of a clean technology ITC would otherwise be available for the property. Include this unpaid amount in the capital cost at the time it is paid (see subsection 127.45(9)).
- **130**: Include government and non-government assistance that you received, that you are entitled to receive, or that you can reasonably expect to receive in respect of the property (see subsection 127.45(5)).
- **135**: Include government and non-government assistance that you repaid (or that you have not received and can no longer reasonably expect to receive) that was applied to reduce the capital cost of a property for a previous tax year (see subsection 127.45(7)).
- **155**: If the labour requirements do not apply to the property, leave column 1M blank (see subsection 127.46(15)).
- **220**: If the property is disposed of to a person who deals at arm's length with the corporation, enter the proceeds of disposition. If the property is disposed of to a person who does not deal at arm's length with the corporation, or the property is converted to a non-clean technology use or is exported from Canada, enter the fair market value.
- **410**: For each covered worker at each designated work site, calculate the number of days they were paid below the prevailing wage requirements. Add these amounts together and enter the result on line 410.
