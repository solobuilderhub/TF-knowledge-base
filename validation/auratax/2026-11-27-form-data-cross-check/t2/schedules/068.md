# Sch 068: Additional Tax on Banks and Life Insurers

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/068.json` and `extras/068.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this schedule if you are a corporation that is a bank or life insurer group member at any time during the tax year and are liable to pay the additional tax under section 123.6 of the Income Tax Act.

• The additional tax on banks and life insurers applies to tax years that end after April 7, 2022. For tax years that include April 7, 2022, the additional tax is prorated based on the number of days after April 7, 2022.

• The additional tax is payable at a rate of 1.5% on the corporation's taxable income or taxable income earned in Canada if the corporation is non-resident for the tax year in excess of a $100 million income deduction:
– If the corporation is not related to any other bank or life insurer group member at the end of the tax year, the corporation is entitled to the full income deduction applied against its taxable income
– Alternatively, if the corporation is related to another bank or life insurer group member at the end of the tax year, the income deduction may be allocated among the related group members for all tax years that end in the same calendar year by agreement in Part 1

• A bank or life insurer group member is a bank, a life insurance corporation that carries on business in Canada, or any financial institution (as defined in subsection 190(1)) that is related to a bank or life insurance corporation that carries on business in Canada.

• Unless otherwise stated, all legislative references are to the federal Income Tax Act and Income Tax Regulations.

• File this schedule with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return.

## Help text on individual lines

- **110**: (If the corporation is not registered, enter "NR")
- **amount = [a1_total_120]**: not to exceed $100 million
- **275**: Your income deduction limit is equal to the following amounts, whichever is the least:
- $100 million, if you are not related to another bank or life insurer group member at the end of the tax year
- amount allocated by the Minister under subsection 123.6(4)
- nil, if you have not filed the allocation agreement and the Minister has made no allocation for the group members
- **300**: Amount 2A x (number of days after April 7, 2022) x 1.5% / (number of days in the tax year)
