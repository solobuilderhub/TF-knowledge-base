# Sch 524: Ontario Specialty Types

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/524.json` and `extras/524.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this schedule to identify the specialty type of a corporation carrying on business in the province of Ontario through a permanent 
  establishment if: 
   - its tax year includes January 1, 2009; 
   - the tax year is the first year after incorporation or amalgamation; or 
   - there is a change to the specialty type. 

• If none of the listed specialty types applies, tick box 99 "Other." 

• Unless otherwise noted, references to sections, subsections, and clauses are from the Taxation Act, 2007 (Ontario).
