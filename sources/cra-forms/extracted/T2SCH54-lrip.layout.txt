· Use this schedule to calculate the balance of the low rate income pool (LRIP) at any time in the tax year if you are a corporation resident in Canada that is:
  ­ a corporation other than a Canadian-controlled private corporation (CCPC) or a deposit insurance corporation (DIC)
  ­ a corporation that elected under subsection 89(11) not to be a CCPC
  ­ a corporation that was a substantive CCPC at any time in the previous tax year starting after April 6, 2022

· When an eligible dividend was paid or there was a change in the LRIP balance in the tax year, file this schedule with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return.

  Do not send your worksheets (pages 3 to 5) with your return, but keep them in case we ask to see them later.

· Unless otherwise stated, all legislative references are to the Income Tax Act.

· Subsection 89(1) defines the terms eligible dividend, excessive eligible dividend designation, general rate income pool, and low rate income pool.

· Subsection 129(4) defines the term aggregate investment income and subsection 248(1) defines the term substantive CCPC.
