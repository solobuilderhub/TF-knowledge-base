· You can claim the Yukon business carbon price rebate (which includes the general business carbon price rebate and the mining business carbon

  price rebate) under section 16 of the Yukon Income Tax Act, if all of the following conditions apply:

  ­ You are an eligible Yukon business taxpayer or an eligible Yukon mining business taxpayer

  ­ You used an eligible Yukon asset or an eligible Yukon mining asset in the tax year, principally in carrying on an eligible Yukon business or an

      eligible Yukon mining business

  ­ Your tax year ended before April 1, 2025

· The Yukon business carbon price rebate applies, for tax years ending before April 1, 2025, to an eligible Yukon business taxpayer and, for tax years ending

  after 2022 and before April 1, 2025, to an eligible Yukon mining business taxpayer.

· The Yukon mining business carbon price rebate is a refundable tax credit equal to the amount of the mining business rebate factor for the financial year in

  which the tax year ends multiplied by the eligible Yukon mining undepreciated capital cost (UCC) to the taxpayer as of the end of the tax year.

· The Yukon general business carbon price rebate is a refundable tax credit equal to the amount of the general business rebate factor for the financial year in

  which the tax year ends multiplied by the amount of the eligible Yukon UCC to the taxpayer as of the end of the tax year.

· The terms eligible Yukon business taxpayer, eligible Yukon mining business taxpayer, eligible Yukon asset, eligible Yukon mining asset, eligible

  Yukon UCC, eligible Yukon mining UCC, eligible Yukon business, eligible Yukon mining business, and cross-border transport equipment are
  defined in subsection 16(1) of the Yukon Income Tax Act.

· To claim the Yukon business carbon price rebate, file a completed copy of this schedule with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return.

   Notes

1 The following classes are prescribed for the purposes of the definition eligible Yukon mining asset:
  (a) Classes 1, 3, 10, 10.1, 13, 16, 22, 28, 29, 38, 39, 40, 41, 41.2, 43, and 53
  (b) Classes 43.1, 43.2, 54, 55, and 56
  The classes prescribed for the purposes of the definition eligible Yukon mining asset are subject to change.
  Where the list of classes provided here differs from that provided in the Carbon Price Rebate General Regulation, the latter takes precedence.

2 The inclusion rates for prescribed classes of eligible Yukon mining assets are:
  (a) Classes 1, 3, 10, 10.1, 13, 16, 22, 28, 29, 38, 39, 40, 41, 41.2, 43, and 53: 100%
  (b) Classes 43.1, 43.2, 54, 55, and 56: for tax years ending before April 1, 2025, 500% multiplied by the general business rebate factor for
      the financial year in which the tax year ends divided by the mining business rebate factor for that financial year.

3 If you don't use cross-border transport equipment, enter "1".
  If you use cross-border transport equipment principally to transport passengers or goods from Yukon to outside Yukon, or you make an election under
  subsection 16(11) of the Yukon Income Tax Act, enter the prescribed fraction.
  The prescribed fraction equals the net fuel quantity you used in Yukon for operating the cross-border transport equipment for the tax year, divided by the net
  fuel quantity you used worldwide (including in Yukon) in operating that equipment.
  Enter a decimal number less than or equal to 1 with a maximum of two decimal places to express this fraction.

4 If you carry on a specified placer mining business in the tax year and do not carry on a specified quartz mining business in the tax year, enter:

 ­ "4" if your tax year ends before April 1, 2024, or
 ­ "2" if your tax year ends after March 31, 2024

 If you carry on a specified quartz mining business in the tax year, enter "1".
5 If you are at any time a member of a partnership that is an eligible Yukon mining business partnership, the eligible Yukon mining UCC from the

  partnership at the end of your tax year that includes that time is equal to your specified proportion of the eligible Yukon mining UCC to the partnership
  at the end of the partnership's fiscal period that includes that time.

  Enter the total of all eligible Yukon mining UCCs provided by the partnerships of which you are a member.

6 If the tax year is shorter than 12 months, prorate the Yukon mining business carbon price rebate or the Yukon general business carbon price rebate by
 the number of days in the tax year divided by 365.

7 Use the mining business rebate factor for the Yukon financial year in which the tax year ends.
  For the purposes of the mining business rebate factor, a financial year does not include a financial year that ends after March 31, 2025.
  The mining business rebate factor is equal to:

 ­ $21.08 for each $1,000 of eligible Yukon mining UCC if your tax year ends before April 1, 2024 (enter 0.02108)
 ­ $36.47 for each $1,000 of eligible Yukon mining UCC if your tax year ends after March 31, 2024 and before April 1, 2025 (enter 0.03647)

8 The following classes are prescribed for the purposes of the definition eligible Yukon asset:
  (a) Classes 1 and 3
  (b) Classes 10, 10.1, 13, 16, 22, 28, 29, 38, 39, 40, 43, and 53
  (c) Classes 43.1, 43.2, 54, 55, and 56
  The classes prescribed for the purposes of the definition eligible Yukon asset are subject to change.
  Where the list of classes provided here differs from that provided in the Carbon Price Rebate General Regulation, the latter takes precedence.

9 The inclusion rates for prescribed classes of eligible Yukon assets are:
  (a) Classes 1 and 3: 50%
  (b) Classes 10, 10.1, 13, 16, 22, 28, 29, 38, 39, 40, 43, and 53: 250%
  (c) Classes 43.1, 43.2, 54, 55, and 56: for tax years ending before April 1, 2025, 500%

10 If you are at any time a member of a partnership that is an eligible Yukon business partnership, the eligible Yukon UCC from the partnership at the
   end of your tax year that includes that time is equal to your specified proportion of the eligible Yukon UCC to the partnership at the end of the
   partnership's fiscal period that includes that time.
   Enter the total of all eligible Yukon UCCs provided by the partnerships of which you are a member.

11 Use the general business rebate factor for the Yukon financial year in which the tax year ends.
  For the purposes of the general business rebate factor, a financial year does not include a financial year that ends after March 31, 2025.
  The general business rebate factor is equal to:

 ­ $21.89 for each $1,000 of eligible Yukon UCC if your tax year ends before April 1, 2024 (enter 0.02189)
 ­ $22.20 for each $1,000 of eligible Yukon UCC if your tax year ends after March 31, 2024 and before April 1, 2025 (enter 0.02220)
