· For use by corporations that are financial institutions with a permanent establishment (as defined in the federal Income Tax Regulations) at any time in the

  tax year in the province of Newfoundland and Labrador, including off-shore areas, and that are liable to pay tax on their capital for tax years starting after
  October 31, 2008.

· Subsection 66.1(1) of the Newfoundland and Labrador Income Tax Act defines the term financial institution.

· You have to complete Schedule 38, Part VI Tax on Capital of Financial Institutions, in order to complete this schedule. File these two completed schedules

  with the T2 Corporation Income Tax Return within six months of the end of the tax year.
