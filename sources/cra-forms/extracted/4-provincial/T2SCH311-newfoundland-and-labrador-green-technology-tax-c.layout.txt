· If you are an eligible corporation that has acquired eligible property, or you are an eligible corporation that has an unused Newfoundland and Labrador green

  technology tax credit at the end of the previous year, use this schedule to claim a tax credit against your Newfoundland and Labrador corporation income tax
  payable. The applicable rate of the tax credit is 20%.

· Credits earned in the year are applied to reduce Newfoundland and Labrador income tax otherwise payable for the year.

· You may be eligible for a refund of up to 40% of your green technology tax credit for a tax year. The amount eligible to be refunded may be reduced by any

  tax, interest, or penalty owing for that or any prior tax year under the federal and provincial income tax acts, and any amount owing under the Canada Pension
  Plan and the Employment Insurance Act. Any remaining balance will be refunded.

· You can carry forward any unused tax credit up to 20 years or carry it back to any of the three previous years. However, you cannot apply it to a tax year that

  ended before April 7, 2022.

· Unless otherwise stated, all legislative references are to the Newfoundland and Labrador Income Tax Act, 2000.

· The tax credit does not apply to an eligible corporation that is exempt from tax under section 149 of the federal Income Tax Act.

· The terms capital cost, eligible corporation, eligible property, and green technology tax credit are defined in subsection 41.2(1).

· To claim the green technology tax credit, file a completed copy of this schedule with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return.
