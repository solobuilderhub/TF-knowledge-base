## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• If you are an eligible corporation that has acquired eligible property, or you are an eligible corporation that has an unused Newfoundland and Labrador manufacturing and processing investment tax credit at the end of the previous year, use this schedule to claim a tax credit against your Newfoundland and Labrador corporation income tax payable. The applicable rate is 10%.

• Credits earned in the year are applied to reduce Newfoundland and Labrador income tax otherwise payable for the year. If you are a Canadian-controlled private corporation (CCPC), you may be eligible for a refund of up to 40% of your manufacturing and processing investment tax credit for a tax year. The amount eligible to be refunded may be reduced by any tax, interest, or penalty owing for that or any prior tax year under the federal and provincial income tax acts, and any amounts owing under the Canada Pension Plan and the Employment Insurance Act. Any remaining balance will be refunded.

• You can carry forward any unused tax credit up to 20 years or carry it back to any of the 3 previous years. However, you cannot apply it to a tax year that ended before April 7, 2022.

• The terms capital cost, eligible corporation, eligible property, and manufacturing and processing investment tax credit, are defined in subsection 41.1(1) of the Newfoundland and Labrador Income Tax Act, 2000.

• Unless otherwise stated, all legislative references are to the federal Income Tax Act.

• To claim the Newfoundland and Labrador manufacturing and processing investment tax credit, file a completed copy of this schedule with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return.

## Help text on individual lines

- **105**: The eligibility date is the date that the property was acquired for and used in a business operating in Newfoundland and Labrador. If the acquisition date and the date when the property was first used in the business are not the same, indicate the most recent date.
- **110**: The cost of acquiring eligible property is the capital cost of the property (excluding amounts added by an election under section 21), determined without reference to subsections 13(7.1) and 13(7.4), minus the amount of any government or non-government assistance that the corporation has received, is entitled to receive, or can reasonably be expected to receive for that property.
- **230**: Enter the amount from line 230 on line 508 of Schedule 5.
- **[a2_next230]**: If the answer is no, enter 0 at line 240
- **amount C [a2_3]**: If negative, enter "0"
- **240**: Enter the amount from line 240 on line 523 of Schedule 5.
- **250**: Amount E cannot exceed amount B. If it does, reduce one of the amounts entered at lines 230, 240, and amount D in order to reduce amount E to a value equal to or less than amount B.
