· Corporations must use this schedule to report:
   ­ non-taxable dividends under section 83
   ­ deductible dividends under subsection 138(6)
   ­ dividends deductible from taxable income under section 112, subsections 113(2) and 138(6), and paragraphs 113(1)(a), (a.1), (b), (c) or (d)

· All legislative references are to the federal Income Tax Act.
· The calculations in this schedule apply only to private or subject corporations (as defined in subsection 186(3)).
· A payer corporation is connected with a recipient corporation at any time in a tax year, if at that time the recipient corporation meets

  either of the following conditions:
   ­ it controls the payer corporation, other than because of a right referred to in paragraph 251(5)(b)
   ­ it owns more than 10% of the issued share capital (with full voting rights), and shares that have a fair market value of more than

      10% of the fair market value of all shares of the payer corporation

     Endnotes

1 If taxable dividends are received, enter the amount in column 240, but if the corporation is not subject to Part IV tax (such as a public corporation other than
  a subject corporation as defined in subsection 186(3)), enter "0" in column 275 (and column 265, if applicable). Life insurers are not subject to Part IV tax on
  subsection 138(6) dividends.

2 Enter "0" if the dividend is paid by a connected payer corporation and yes is selected in column 243. Do not include dividends paid by the payer
  corporation, in its tax year in which it paid the dividend, that are suspended dividends under subsection 129(1.3). For more information, see
  subparagraph 186(1)(b)(ii).

3 Enter "0" if the dividend is paid by a connected payer corporation and yes is selected in column 243. If the connected payer corporation's tax year ends
  after the corporation's balance-due day for the tax year (two or three months, as applicable), you have to estimate the payer's dividend refund when you
  calculate the corporation's Part IV tax payable. Do not include dividends paid by the payer corporation, in its tax year in which it paid the dividend, that are
  suspended dividends under subsection 129(1.3). For more information, see subparagraph 186(1)(b)(i).

4 Enter "0" if the dividend is paid by a connected payer corporation and yes is selected in column 243. For eligible dividends received from connected
  corporations, Part IV tax on dividends is equal to column 260 divided by column 250 multiplied by column 242. Do not include any taxable dividend
  deemed to be paid by the payer corporation under subsection 129(1.32) in the dividend refund calculation.

5 Enter "0" if the dividend is paid by a connected payer corporation and yes is selected in column 243. For taxable dividends received from connected
  corporations, Part IV tax on dividends is equal to column 260 divided by column 250 multiplied by column 240. Do not include any taxable dividend
  deemed to be paid by the payer corporation under subsection 129(1.32) in the dividend refund calculation.

6 Enter "0" if the dividend is paid by a connected payer corporation and yes is selected in column 243. For the purpose of calculating your eligible refundable
  dividend tax on hand (ERDTOH), Part IV tax on taxable dividends received from connected corporations (with a tax year starting after 2018) is equal to the
  sum of Part IV tax on eligible dividends and non-eligible dividends received from connected corporations to the extent that such dividends caused a dividend
  refund to those corporations from their ERDTOH.

  Part IV tax before deductions on taxable dividends received from connected corporations for purposes of column 280 is the lesser of the Part IV tax
  applicable to such taxable dividends and the sum of (i) and (ii), where:

      (i) Part IV tax on eligible dividends received from connected corporations is equal to amount CC of the connected payer corporation (on page 7 of the
          T2 return) divided by line 465 of the connected payer corporation, multiplied by column 242 and

      (ii) Part IV tax on non-eligible dividends received from connected corporations is equal to amount II of the connected payer corporation (on page 7 of
           the T2 return) divided by line 470 of the connected payer corporation, multiplied by the difference between columns 240 and 242

7 Do not include amounts deemed to have been paid in the tax year under subsection 129(1.32). Report those at line 458 only.
