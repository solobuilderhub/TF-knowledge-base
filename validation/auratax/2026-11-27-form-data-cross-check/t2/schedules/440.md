# Sch 440: Yukon Manufacturing and Processing Profits Tax Credit

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/440.json` and `extras/440.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this schedule if you had a permanent establishment (under section 400 of the federal Income Tax Regulations) in Yukon at any time in the tax
  year, and had:
   – taxable income earned in the tax year in Yukon
   – Canadian manufacturing and processing profits, under subsection 125.1(3) of the federal Income Tax Act, earned in the tax year in Yukon

• This schedule is a worksheet only and is not required to be filed with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return.

## Help text on individual lines

- **amount 1 [a1_5]**: Applies only to corporations that were Canadian-controlled private corporations throughout the tax year.
- **amount 2 [a1_6]**: Applies only to corporations that were Canadian-controlled private corporations throughout the tax year.
- **amount 3 [a1_7]**: Calculate the amount of foreign business income tax credit without reference to the corporate tax reductions under section 123.4 of the
federal Income Tax Act.
- **amount G [a1_10]**: If your corporation had a permanent establishment only in Yukon, enter the taxable income from line 360 of the T2 return. Otherwise,
enter the taxable income allocated to Yukon from column F in Part 1 of Schedule 5, Tax Calculation Supplementary – Corporations.

Includes the territories and the offshore jurisdictions for Nova Scotia and Newfoundland and Labrador.
- **amount I [a1_12]**: If your corporation had a permanent establishment only in Yukon, enter the taxable income from line 360 of the T2 return. Otherwise,
enter the taxable income allocated to Yukon from column F in Part 1 of Schedule 5, Tax Calculation Supplementary – Corporations.

Includes the territories and the offshore jurisdictions for Nova Scotia and Newfoundland and Labrador.
- **amount J [a1_13]**: Enter amount J on line 677 of Schedule 5
