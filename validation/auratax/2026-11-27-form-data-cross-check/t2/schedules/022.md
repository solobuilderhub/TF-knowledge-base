From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/022.json` and `extras/022.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

Complete this schedule if, at any time during the tax year, one of the following organizations had a beneficial interest in a
non-resident discretionary trust:
• the corporation;
• a controlled foreign affiliate of the corporation; or
• any other corporation or trust that did not deal at arm's length with the corporation.
