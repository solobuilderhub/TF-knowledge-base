# Sch 481: Nunavut Corporation Tax Calculation

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/481.json` and `extras/481.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this schedule if your corporation had a permanent establishment (as defined in section 400 of the federal Income Tax Regulations) in Nunavut,
  and had taxable income earned in the year in Nunavut.
• This schedule is a worksheet only and is not required to be filed with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return.

## Help text on individual lines

- **amount 1A [a1_1]**: If your corporation had a permanent establishment only in Nunavut, enter the taxable income from line 360 of the T2 return. Otherwise, enter the 
taxable income allocated to Nunavut from column F in Part 1 of Schedule 5, Tax Calculation Supplementary – Corporations.
- **amount 2C [a2_3]**: If your corporation had a permanent establishment in more than one jurisdiction, or is claiming a Nunavut tax credit, enter amount 2C on line 260 
of Schedule 5. Otherwise, enter it on line 760 of the T2 return.
