# Sch 427: British Columbia Corporation Tax Calculation

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/427.json` and `extras/427.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this schedule if your corporation had a permanent establishment (as defined in section 400 of the federal Income Tax Regulations) British Columbia and had taxable income earned in the year in British Columbia.

• This schedule is a worksheet only and is not required to be filed with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return.

## Help text on individual lines

- **amount 1A [a1_1]**: If your corporation had a permanent establishment only in British Columbia, enter the taxable income from line 360 of the T2 return.
Otherwise, enter the taxable income allocated to British Columbia from column F in Part 1 of Schedule 5, Tax Calculation Supplementary – Corporations.
- **amount 1H [a1_8]**: if negative, enter 0
- **amount 2C [a2_3]**: If your corporation had a permanent establishment in more than one jurisdiction or is claiming a British Columbia tax credit, enter amount 2C
on line 240 of Schedule 5. Otherwise, enter it on line 760 of the T2 return.
