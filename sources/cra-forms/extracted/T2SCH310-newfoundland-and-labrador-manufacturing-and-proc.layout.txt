· If you are an eligible corporation that has acquired eligible property, or you are an eligible corporation that has an unused Newfoundland and Labrador

   manufacturing and processing investment tax credit at the end of the previous year, use this schedule to claim a tax credit against your Newfoundland and
   Labrador corporation income tax payable. The applicable rate is 10%.

· Credits earned in the year are applied to reduce Newfoundland and Labrador income tax otherwise payable for the year. If you are a Canadian-controlled

   private corporation (CCPC), you may be eligible for a refund of up to 40% of your manufacturing and processing investment tax credit for a tax year. The
   amount eligible to be refunded may be reduced by any tax, interest, or penalty owing for that or any prior tax year under the federal and provincial income tax
   acts, and any amounts owing under the Canada Pension Plan and the Employment Insurance Act. Any remaining balance will be refunded.

· You can carry forward any unused tax credit up to 20 years or carry it back to any of the 3 previous years. However, you cannot apply it to a tax year that

   ended before April 7, 2022.

· The terms capital cost, eligible corporation, eligible property, and manufacturing and processing investment tax credit, are defined in

   subsection 41.1(1) of the Newfoundland and Labrador Income Tax Act, 2000.

· Unless otherwise stated, all legislative references are to the federal Income Tax Act.
· To claim the Newfoundland and Labrador manufacturing and processing investment tax credit, file a completed copy of this schedule with your T2

   Corporation Income Tax Return.
