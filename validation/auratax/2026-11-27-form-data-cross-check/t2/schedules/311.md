# Sch 311: Newfoundland and Labrador Green Technology Tax Credit

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/311.json` and `extras/311.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• If you are an eligible corporation that has acquired eligible property, or you are an eligible corporation that has an unused Newfoundland and Labrador green technology tax credit at the end of the previous year, use this schedule to claim a tax credit against your Newfoundland and Labrador corporation income tax payable. The applicable rate of the tax credit is 20%.

• Credits earned in the year are applied to reduce Newfoundland and Labrador income tax otherwise payable for the year.

• You may be eligible for a refund of up to 40% of your green technology tax credit for a tax year. The amount eligible to be refunded may be reduced by any tax, interest, or penalty owing for that or any prior tax year under the federal and provincial income tax acts, and any amount owing under the Canada Pension Plan and the Employment Insurance Act. Any remaining balance will be refunded.

• You can carry forward any unused tax credit up to 20 years or carry it back to any of the three previous years. However, you cannot apply it to a tax year that ended before April 7, 2022.

• Unless otherwise stated, all legislative references are to the Newfoundland and Labrador Income Tax Act, 2000.

• The tax credit does not apply to an eligible corporation that is exempt from tax under section 149 of the federal Income Tax Act.

• The terms capital cost, eligible corporation, eligible property, and green technology tax credit are defined in subsection 41.2(1).

• To claim the green technology tax credit, file a completed copy of this schedule with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return.

## Help text on individual lines

- **105**: The identification number is the business number of the eligible corporation.
- **120**: The amount on line 120 is equal to one of the following two amounts, whichever is applicable:
– $1 million multiplied by the percentage assigned to your corporation in column 3
– nil, if the total of column 3 is more than 100%, or you are associated in the tax year with one or more other corporations and did not file an agreement in prescribed form as required under subparagraph 41.2(2)(b)(ii)
- **200**: Only Classes 43.1 or 43.2 of Schedule II to the federal Income Tax Regulations, which fall within the definition of eligible property, are to be reported in this column.
- **205**: The eligibility date is the date that the property was acquired for and used in a business operating in Newfoundland and Labrador. If the acquisition date and the date when the asset was first used in the business are not the same, indicate the most recent date.
- **210**: The amount reported in this column is the capital cost to the eligible corporation of each eligible property acquired before the time of the undepreciated capital cost calculation.
- **amount B [a3_2]**: The tax credit limit is equal to one of the following two amounts, whichever is applicable:
– $1 million, if you are not associated with any other corporations in the tax year within the meaning of section 256 of the federal Income Tax Act
– amount from line 120 in Part 1, if you are associated with one or more other corporations in the tax year within the meaning of section 256 of the federal Income Tax Act
- **amount C [a3_3]**: line 315 or amount B, whichever is less
- **amount D [a3_4]**: line 310 plus amount C
- **330**: Enter the amount from line 330 on line 506 of Schedule 5.
- **amount E [a3_5]**: If the amount is negative, enter "0".
- **340**: Enter the amount from line 340 on line 526 of Schedule 5.
- **amount F [a3_6]**: complete Part 4
- **350**: Amount G cannot exceed amount D. If it does, reduce one of the amounts entered at lines 330, 340, and amount F in order to reduce amount G to a value equal to or less than amount D.
