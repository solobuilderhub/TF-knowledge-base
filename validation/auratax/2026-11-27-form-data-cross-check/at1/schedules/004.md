## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

For corporations which have included in income any foreign investment income and which are entitled to a Federal Non-Business Foreign  Tax Credit.

Report all monetary values in dollars; DO NOT include cents.

## Help text on individual lines

- **amount C [a1_T1_C3]**: If the corporation has permanent establishments in Alberta only, enter "1" in column C.
- **[a1_T1_ACTA]**: Enter the deduction from income under subsection 8(2.2) of the Alberta Corporate Tax Act (ACTA) for the country only when it differs from the deduction under subsection 20(12) of the Income Tax Act (ITA), federal schedule 21 line 130. Column E is then federal schedule 21 line 120 minus the greater of the two, and the total of the Alberta amounts for each country is included as a deduction in line 040 of Alberta Schedule 12 (the federal line 130 amounts in line 041). This amount is not filed with the return.
- **006**: If the corporation's deduction from income under subsection 8(2.2) of the Alberta Corporate Tax Act (ACTA) is different from the deduction under subsection 20(12) of the Income Tax Act (ITA) for any country, then Alberta Schedule 12 is required to be  completed. The total of these amounts for each country for Alberta purposes is to be included in the amount at line 040 on  Alberta Schedule 12. See guide for more information.
- **amount 020 [a1_020]**: Enter this amount on AT1 page 2, line 072
