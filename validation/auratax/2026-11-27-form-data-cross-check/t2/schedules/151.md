# Sch 151: Investment Revenue from Designated Insurance Property for Insurance Companies

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/151.json` and `extras/151.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this schedule to calculate amounts for the mean Canadian investment fund, gross investment revenue from designated investment property, and the minimum revenue test for income tax purposes from an insurance business carried on in Canada by non-resident insurers, all resident life insurance companies carrying on an insurance business in Canada and a country other than Canada and resident fraternal benefit societies carrying on an insurance business in Canada and a country other than Canada.

• This schedule constitutes prescribed information in prescribed form as required to be filed with the Minister as part of the return of income for the tax year as set out in subsection 150(1) of the Income Tax Act.

• As of 2023, net income or loss from Canadian operations is reported on page 70.022 of the Life Supervisory Quarterly return or page 92.22 of the P&C Supervisory Quarterly return (find more information about this return at osfi-bsif.gc.ca). Fraternal benefit societies should only include net income or loss from the insurance funds. Activity in fraternal and other funds should be omitted.

• All insurance companies and fraternal benefit societies carrying on business in Canada need to complete Schedule 150, Net Income (loss) for Income Tax Purposes for Insurance Companies.

• All resident companies and fraternal benefit societies operating a life insurance business in Canada and a country other than Canada, and all non-resident companies operating an insurance business in Canada need to complete Schedule 151.

• All legislative references are to the Income Tax Act or where appropriate, the Income Tax Regulations.

• File the following documents with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return:
  – a copy of this schedule
  – a copy of schedule T2 SCH 150
