# Sch 027: Calculation of Canadian Manufacturing and Processing Profits Deduction

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/027.json` and `extras/027.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this schedule to claim either the Canadian manufacturing and processing profits deduction (including for profits from the generation of electrical energy for sale or production of steam for sale) or the zero-emission technology manufacturing (ZETM) deduction.

• Corporations claiming the manufacturing and processing profits deduction should begin with parts 1, 2, or 10, as applicable:
– small manufacturing corporations that meet all requirements in Part 1 should begin with Part 1
– corporations that only generate electrical energy for sale or produce steam for sale should begin with Part 10 on page 5
– all other corporations should begin with Part 2

• Corporations claiming the ZETM deduction must complete Part 14 on page 7.

• All legislative references are to the federal Income Tax Act and Income Tax Regulations.

• Income that is eligible for the small business deduction is not eligible for the manufacturing and processing profits deduction, but may be eligible for the ZETM deduction if related to relevant eligible activities.

• See page 9 for notes and definitions to help you complete this schedule.
