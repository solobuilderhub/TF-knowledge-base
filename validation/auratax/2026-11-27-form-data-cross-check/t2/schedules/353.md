| [a1_1] | • Use this schedule if you are related financial institutions that are liable to pay the Nova Scotia financial institutions capital tax to allocate the basic capital deduction among the members of the related group. <br>	– $5 million, if the total amount of the capital of the financial institution and its related financial institutions is $10 million or less<br>	– $30 million, for a financial institution that is a trust company or a loan company with its registered office in Nova Scotia<br>	– the deduction is NIL in all other circumstances.<br>• A related financial institution that has more than one tax year ending in a calendar year has to file an agreement for each tax year ending in that calendar year. <br>• A financial institution that is related to one or more other financial institutions at the end of a taxation year may file this agreement for the allocation of the basic capital deduction on behalf of the related group of which the financial institution is a member, for the taxation year. The amount allocated cannot be more than the basic capital deduction.<br>• Subsections 190.15(5), and (6) apply to this allocation if the references of capital deduction and corporation are read as basic capital deduction and financial institution respectively.<br>• According to subsection 190.15(5), when a corporation has more than one tax year ending in the same calendar year and is related in two or more of those tax years to another corporation that has a tax year ending in that calendar year, the basic capital deduction of the first corporation for each such tax year at the end of which it is related to the other corporation is an amount equal to its basic capital deduction for the first such tax year.<br>• Unless otherwise stated, all legislative references are to the federal Income Tax Act.<br>• If you do not have enough space to list all the information, use an additional Schedule 353.<br>• File a completed copy of this schedule with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return.  |  | heading |  |  |  |
