# Sch 078: Carbon Capture, Utilization, and Storage Investment Tax Credit

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/078.json` and `extras/078.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this schedule to calculate your carbon capture, utilization, and storage investment tax credit (CCUS ITC) if you are a taxable Canadian corporation or a taxable Canadian corporation that is a member of a partnership that has incurred eligible expenses after 2021 and before 2041.

• File a separate schedule for each project that is assigned a project code by Natural Resources Canada (NRCan).

• The terms dual-use equipment, eligible use, first day of commercial operations, non-government assistance, projected eligible use percentage, qualified CCUS expenditure, specified percentage, and total CCUS project review period are defined in subsection 127.44(1).

• The terms apprenticeship requirements, covered worker, designated work site, installation taxation year, prevailing wage requirements, Red Seal trade, Red Seal worker, reduced tax credit rate, regular tax credit rate, and specified property are defined in subsection 127.46(1).

• The specified percentages to calculate your cumulative CCUS development tax credit or your CCUS refurbishment tax credit for a tax year are:
  - Carbon capture (ambient air) : After 2021 and before 2036 (60%), After 2035 and before 2041 (30%), After 2040 (0%).
  - Carbon capture (other) : After 2021 and before 2036 (50%), After 2035 and before 2041 (25%), After 2040 (0%).
  - Carbon transportation : After 2021 and before 2036 (37.5%), After 2035 and before 2041 (18.75%), After 2040 (0%).
  - Carbon storage : After 2021 and before 2036 (37.5%), After 2035 and before 2041 (18.75%), After 2040 (0%).
  - Carbon use : After 2021 and before 2036 (37.5%), After 2035 and before 2041 (18.75%), After 2040 (0%).

• Labour requirements apply in respect of specified property prepared or installed after November 27, 2023. If you do not elect to meet the labour requirements in Part 2, you will only be able to claim the CCUS ITC at the reduced tax credit rate, which is the regular tax credit rate (specified percentage) minus 10 percentage points.

• Expenditures incurred before the first day of commercial operations are eligible for the CCUS development tax credit. Expenditures incurred on or after the first day of commercial operations and during the total CCUS project review period are eligible for the CCUS refurbishment tax credit. Expenditures that are eligible for the CCUS refurbishment tax credit are subject to a limit of 10% of the total of all qualified CCUS expenditures incurred by the taxpayer before the first day of commercial operations of the CCUS project.

• Generally, you may claim only one of the clean economy ITCs for the same eligible property. You may claim multiple clean economy ITCs for the same project, if the project includes different types of eligible property. Additionally, you cannot claim both the CCUS ITC and the ITCs in section 127 (for example, the Atlantic ITC) for the same qualified expenditure.

• All legislative references are to the Income Tax Act.

• File this schedule with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return for each year of the CCUS project on or before the later of December 31, 2026, and one year after your filing due date for the tax year.

• For more information, go to canada.ca/clean-economy-credits.

• If you need more space, attach additional sheets.

## Help text on individual lines

- **120**: Enter the province, assign a number to each designated work site and keep a record in case the Canada Revenue Agency asks to see it later.
- **122**: Assign a number to each designated work site and keep a record in case the Canada Revenue Agency asks to see it later. If there is more than one designated work site associated with the property, you must elect to meet the labour requirements for each designated work site to claim at the regular tax credit rate.
- **125**: If any part of the qualified expenditure of the CCUS project is unpaid 180 days after the end of the tax year in which the expenditure is otherwise incurred, the expenditure is deemed to be incurred at the time it is paid (see subsection 127.44(12)). Also see special rules in subsection 127.44(9).
- **130**: Include non-government assistance that you received, that you are entitled to receive, or that you can reasonably expect to receive in respect of the property (see subsection 127.44(9)).
- **135**: Include non-government assistance that you repaid (or that you have not received and can no longer reasonably expect to receive) that was applied to reduce the capital cost of a property for a preceding tax year (see subsection 127.44(10)).
- **145**: If the entry in column 1B is carbon capture or carbon transportation, enter the result of the formula [(B+C+D+E)xF] for projected eligible use described under qualified carbon capture expenditure or qualified carbon transportation expenditure in subsection 127.44(1). Otherwise, enter "1".
- **155**: The specified percentages to calculate your cumulative CCUS development tax credit or your CCUS refurbishment tax credit for a tax year are
- After 2021 and before 2036 : Carbon capture (ambient air) 60%, Carbon capture (other)  50%, Carbon transportation 37.5, Carbon storage 37.5% Carbon use 37.5%
- After 2035 and before 2041 : Carbon capture (ambient air) 30%, Carbon capture (other)  25%, Carbon transportation 18.75, Carbon storage 18.75% Carbon use 18.75%
After 2040 : 0% for all.
- **170**: If the projected eligible use percentage is reduced prior to the first day of commercial operations, recalculate the cumulative CCUS development tax credit for the current year (see subsection 127.44(6)).
- **180**: If this amount is negative, enter "0" and fill out Part 4 (see subsection 211.92(2)).
- **amount A [a1_lA]**: Enter amount A on line 200 of Schedule 31, Investment Tax Credit - Corporations
- **310**: For each covered worker at each designated work site, calculate the number of days they were paid below the prevailing wage requirements. Add these amounts together and enter the result on line 310.
- **312**: For any part of a tax year after December 31, 2023, adjust this amount for inflation using the consumer price index and prorate it accordingly (see subsection 127.46(8)). For more information, go to canada.ca/clean-economy-credits-employer-compliance.
- **[a3_subtitle2]**: The maximum percentage of apprentices to journeypersons is based on the applicable law or collective agreement, (see paragraph 127.46(5)(b)).
- **322**: For any part of a tax year after December 31, 2023, adjust this amount for inflation using the consumer price index and prorate it accordingly (see subsection 127.46(8)). For more information, go to canada.ca/clean-economy-credits-employer-compliance.
- **330**: Enter the amount from line 330 on line 580 of the T2 return.
- **004**: Columns 004 and 005 satisfy the requirements under subsection 211.93(7).
- **[a4_title2]**: This amount is calculated for a recovery taxation year if the actual eligible use percentage for the relevant project period is more than 5 percentage points lower than the projected eligible use percentage of the CCUS project for that period. If the actual eligible use percentage for any period described in subparagraph (c)(i) or (ii) of the definition of qualified CCUS project is less than 10%, then the actual eligible use percentage of the project for the relevant project period to which the period relates (and for each subsequent project period) is NIL. Additionally, the relevant project period for the particular recovery taxation year is deemed to include each subsequent project period (see subsection 211.92(3)).
- **[a4_title3]**: This amount is calculated for a recovery taxation year if the actual eligible use percentage for the relevant project period is more than 5 percentage points lower than the projected eligible use percentage of the CCUS project for that period. If the actual eligible use percentage for any period described in subparagraph (c)(i) or (ii) of the definition of qualified CCUS project is less than 10%, then the actual eligible use percentage of the project for the relevant project period to which the period relates (and for each subsequent project period) is NIL. Additionally, the relevant project period for the particular recovery taxation year is deemed to include each subsequent project period (see subsection 211.92(3)).
- **430**: If the property is disposed of to a person who deals at arm's length with the corporation, enter the proceeds of disposition. If the property is disposed of to a person who does not deal at arm's length with the corporation, or is exported from Canada but not disposed of, enter the fair market value. The amount cannot exceed the capital cost of the property.
- **amount J [a4_lL]**: Enter amount J on line 726 of the T2 return.
