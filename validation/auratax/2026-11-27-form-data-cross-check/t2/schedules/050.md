# Sch 050: Shareholder Information

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/050.json` and `extras/050.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• All private corporations must complete this schedule for any shareholder who holds 10% or more of the corporation's common and/or 
  preferred shares.

• Provide only one number (business number, partnership account number, social insurance number or trust number) per shareholder.

## Help text on individual lines

- **100**: After name, indicate in brackets if the shareholder is a corporation, partnership, individual, or trust.
- **200**: 9 digits, 2 letters, and 4 digits. 
If not registered, enter "NR".
- **300**: 9 digits.

If the shareholder is an individual who does not have a social insurance number (SIN), NA must be entered to indicate the SIN is not available
- **350**: T followed by 8 digits.

If the shareholder is a trust who does not have a trust number, NA must be entered to indicate the trust number is not available.
