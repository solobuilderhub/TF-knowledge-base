# Sch 063: Return of Fuel Charge Proceeds to Farmers Tax Credit

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/063.json` and `extras/063.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this schedule to claim the return of fuel charge proceeds to farmers tax credit if you are a corporation or a corporation that is a member of a partnership that operates a farming business, and have incurred farming expenses of at least $25,000 (subject to exclusions), with one or more permanent establishments in one or more of the following designated provinces: Ontario (ON), Manitoba (MB), Saskatchewan (SK) or Alberta (AB), and additionally, for 2023 and later years, Newfoundland and Labrador (NL), Prince Edward Island (PE), Nova Scotia (NS) or New Brunswick (NB).

• The return of fuel charge proceeds to farmers tax credit is a refundable tax credit equal to the eligible farming expenses attributable to a designated province for the tax year, multiplied by the payment rate for the calendar year for the designated province. Members of a partnership can claim their portion of the partnership's total credit based on their specified proportion.

• If you are operating in more than one province or territory, where at least one is a designated province, eligible farming expenses will be allocated to each designated province based on the relevant proportion under which taxable income is allocated under Part IV of the Income Tax Regulations.

• Unless otherwise stated, all legislative references are to the federal Income Tax Act.

• The terms designated province, eligible farming expenses, payment rate and relevant proportion are defined in subsection 127.42(1).

• To claim this credit, file this schedule with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return.
