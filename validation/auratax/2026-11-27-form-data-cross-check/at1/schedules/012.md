# Sch 012: Income/Loss Reconciliation

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/at1/specs/012.json` and `extras/012.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

The Alberta Corporate Tax Act adopts most rules of the federal Income Tax Act for determining income and taxable income. However, some of the rules are elective and allow corporations to claim different amounts for Alberta purposes than they have for federal purposes. This results in different discretionary Alberta and federal tax account balances. The differences between the Alberta and federal resource tax regimes from 2003 onwards also lead to different income federally and for Alberta purposes. If the corporation elects to differ its claim for Alberta purposes in the current year, if the opening balances for Alberta and federal purposes differ, or if the corporation has crown charges, Alberta Royalty Tax Credit claims or resource allowance in 2003 and onwards, then Schedule 12 MUST be completed and submitted with the applicable supporting Alberta schedule(s).

Report all monetary amounts in dollars; DO NOT indicate cents.  Show negative amounts in brackets (  ).

## Help text on individual lines

- **040**: The program enters here the amounts Alberta Schedules 15 and 18 carry to this line (Schedule 15 negative amounts available, Schedule 18 lines 076 and 094) less the total ACTA 8(2.2) deductions of Alberta Schedule 4 when they differ from the ITA 20(12) deductions. To include other discretionary items (for example foreign affiliate deductible losses that differ for Alberta purposes), use the manual override and enter the whole amount of the line, and explain it at line 048.
- **082**: The program enters Schedule 21 line 017 or, without Schedule 21, the federal amount of line 083 (T2 line 355), the maximum. Without Schedule 21, to add a lower amount (only to the extent that the Alberta small business deduction or the Alberta foreign investment income tax credit would increase), use the manual override and enter it; it cannot exceed line 083.
- **091**: If there is an amount at line 083 and line 002 - line 081 is negative, then line 091 must equal line 083
- **090**: If there is an amount at line 082 and line 054 - line 080 is negative, then line 090 must equal line 082
- **102**: If the calculated amount is negative, enter the negative amount in brackets ( ). If this includes
specified partnership income, ensure the correct Alberta small business threshold is used (See Guide)
