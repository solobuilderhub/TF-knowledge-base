## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

The AT1 and applicable schedules must be received by Tax and Revenue Administration  001 (TRA) within 6 months of the corporation’s taxation year end. Refer to form AT100 to  determine if the corporation is exempt from filing. If the corporation is not exempt from filing  and its gross revenue exceeds $1 million, the corporation must file electronically using net file unless it is an insurance corporation, a non-resident corporation, or reports in functional  004 currency.

Report all monetary amounts in dollars; DO NOT include cents.

Show negative amounts in brackets ( ).

## Help text on individual lines

- **034**: Enter the 9 or 10 digit account number
- **[TI_l1]**: The calculation of taxable income for federal purposes can differ from the calculation for  Alberta purposes if the corporation chooses to use different discretionary deduction amounts (e.g., different application of losses, CCA, charitable donation, etc.).
- **062**: Where line 062 comes from:

- Lines 060 and 061 both "No", with a linked federal T2: calculated as federal T2 line 360 (the form's "lines 360 - 370": the current T2 has no line 370) OR, if reporting a loss, federal Schedule 4 lines 110 + 310.
- Lines 060 and 061 both "No", no linked federal T2: enter the amount yourself (federal T2 line 360, or the loss from federal Schedule 4 lines 110 + 310).
- Line 060 or 061 "Yes": Schedule 12 line 090 (Taxable Income for Alberta purposes). Schedule 12 is added to the return automatically; until it is, an amount entered here is kept.

(If line 062 is negative, complete Schedule 10 to request a loss carry-back, if applicable)

(Show a loss amount in brackets)
- **065**: 1 when all of the corporation's permanent establishments are in Alberta and Schedule 2 is not attached. Otherwise, the Alberta Allocation Factor of Schedule 2, column I (calculated to 6 decimal places): if Schedule 2 is attached but gives no allocation, line 065 stays blank - complete Schedule 2, or remove it when all permanent establishments are in Alberta.
- **amount 066 [TI_066]**: If the corporation has permanent establishments only in Alberta, multiply by "1").

If negative, enter "0".
- **090**: An assessed balance, including interest and penalty charges, of less than $20.00 will be neither charged nor refunded.
- **091**: If line 090 is a balance due (i.e. positive amount), indicate the amount enclosed with the return
Make cheque payable to Government of Alberta
Defaults to 0 for a Net File return
- **092**: If line 090 is an overpayment (i.e. negative amount), indicate the desired disposition
