# Sch 056: Part II.2 Tax on Repurchases of Equity

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/056.json` and `extras/056.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this schedule to calculate Part II.2 tax on repurchases of equity if you are a corporation resident in Canada (other than a mutual fund corporation) that redeemed, acquired or cancelled its own equity listed on a designated stock exchange in the tax year.

• The Part II.2 tax on repurchases of equity applies to transactions that occur after December 31, 2023.

• The terms equity, qualifying issuance, reorganization transaction, specified affiliate, and substantive debt are defined in subsection 183.3(1).

• All legislative references are to the Income Tax Act.

• File a completed copy of this schedule with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return.

## Help text on individual lines

- **200**: Do not include equity that was redeemed, acquired or cancelled in a reorganization transaction, or acquired from a specified affiliate if that equity was previously deemed by subsection 183.3(5) to have been acquired by the corporation and was previously included on line 200.
- **amount A [a1_1]**: A reorganization transaction that is described in paragraph (a) or (b) of the definition of reorganization transaction in subsection 183.3(1).
- **amount B [a1_2]**: Only include the FMV of any equity consideration that is described in paragraph (a) or (b) of the definition of reorganization transaction in subsection 183.3(1) and received in a reorganization transaction that is described in that same paragraph.
- **amount E [a1_5]**: Applies only to equity (other than substantive debt) of the corporation that is disposed of in the tax year by a specified affiliate of the corporation (other than to the corporation or another specified affiliate of the corporation), if that equity was previously deemed by subsection 183.3(5) to have been acquired by the corporation and was previously included on line 200.
- **amount G [a1_7]**: If amount C is less than $1 million (prorated based on the number of days in the tax year if less than 365 days), no Part II.2 tax is payable. In this case, enter "0" at amount G.
