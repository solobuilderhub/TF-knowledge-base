# Sch 150: Net Income (loss) for Income Tax Purposes for Insurance Companies

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/150.json` and `extras/150.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this schedule to reconcile the net income or loss and extraordinary items from an insurance business carried on in Canada by non-resident insurers, all resident insurance companies and fraternal benefit societies operating an insurance business in Canada.

• This schedule constitutes prescribed information in prescribed form as required to be filed with the Minister as part of the return of income for the tax year as set out in subsection 150(1) of the Income Tax Act.

• As of 2023, net income or loss from Canadian operations is reported on page 70.022 of the Life Supervisory Quarterly return or page 92.22 of the P&C Supervisory Quarterly return (find more information about this return at osfi-bsif.gc.ca). Fraternal benefit societies should only include net income or loss from life insurance funds. Activity in fraternal and other funds should be omitted.

• As of 2023, Insurance Contracts Liability roll forward from Canadian operations is reported on pages 70.012 and 70.014 of the Life Supervisory Quarterly return or page 92.12 and 92.14 of the P&C Supervisory Quarterly return (find more information about this return at osfi-bsif.gc.ca).

• As of 2023, Reinsurance Contracts Held Liability roll forward from Canadian operations is reported on pages 70.016 and 70.018 of the Life Supervisory Quarterly return or page 92.16 and 92.18 of the P&C Supervisory Quarterly return (find more information about this return at osfi-bsif.gc.ca).

• All insurance companies and fraternal benefit societies carrying on business in Canada need to complete Schedule 150.

• All resident companies and fraternal benefit societies operating a life insurance business in Canada and a country other than Canada, and all non-resident companies operating an insurance business in Canada need to complete Schedule 151, Investment Revenue From Designated Insurance Property for Insurance Companies

• All legislative references are to the Income Tax Act or where appropriate, the Income Tax Regulations.

• File the following documents with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return:
  – a copy of this schedule
  – a copy of T2 SCH 151
