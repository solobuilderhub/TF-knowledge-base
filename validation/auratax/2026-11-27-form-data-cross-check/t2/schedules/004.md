## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this form to determine the continuity and use of available losses; to determine a current-year non-capital loss, farm loss, restricted farm loss, limited partnership loss, or restricted interest and financing expense; to determine the amount of restricted farm losses, limited partnership losses, and restricted interest and financing expenses (RIFE) that can be applied in a year; and to ask for a loss carryback to previous years.

• A corporation can choose whether or not to deduct an available loss from income in a tax year. The corporation can deduct losses in any order. However, for each type of loss, deduct the oldest loss first.

• All legislative references are to the Income Tax Act.

• According to subsection 111(4), when control has been acquired, no amount of capital loss incurred in a tax year ending before that time is deductible when calculating taxable income for a tax year ending after that time. Also, no amount of capital loss incurred in a tax year ending after that time is deductible when calculating taxable income for a tax year ending before that time.

• When control has been acquired, subsection 111(5) provides for similar treatment of non-capital and farm losses except as listed in paragraphs 111(5)(a) and (b), and of RIFE except as listed in paragraph 111(5)(a).

• For information on these losses, see Guide T4012, T2 Corporation – Income Tax.

• File this schedule with the T2 Corporation Income Tax Return, or send the schedule by itself to the tax centre where the return is filed.

## Help text on individual lines

- **amount 1I [a1_9]**: If positive, enter "0"
- **amount 1M [a1_13]**: If positive enter "0". If amount 1M is negative, enter it on line 110 as a positive amount.
- **100**: A non-capital loss expires after 20 tax years and an allowable business investment loss becomes a net capital loss after 10 tax years.
- **105**: Subsidiary is defined in subsection 88(1) as a taxable Canadian corporation of which 90% or more of each class of issued shares are owned by its parent corporation and the remaining shares are owned by persons that deal at arm's length with the parent corporation.
- **135**: Line 135 is the total of lines 330 and 335 from Schedule 3, Dividends Received, Taxable Dividends Paid, and Part IV Tax Calculation.
- **amount 2D [a2_4]**: Determine the amount of the non-capital loss from the 11th previous tax year, and enter the part of the non-capital loss that was not deducted in the previous 11 years.
- **amount 2E [a2_5]**: Enter the amount of the ABILs from the 11th previous tax year. Enter the full amount on amount 2E.
- **225**: To get the net capital losses required to reduce the taxable capital gain included in the net income (loss) for the current tax year, enter the
amount from line 225 divided by 2 at line 332 of the T2 return.
- **[a2_L2]**: On line 225, 951, 952, or 953, whichever applies, enter the actual amount of the loss. When the loss is applied, divide this amount by 2. The result represents the 50% inclusion rate.
- **280**: Capital losses can be carried forward indefinitely.
- **300**: A farm loss expires after 20 tax years.
- **335**: Line 335 is the total of lines 340 and 345 from Schedule 3.
- **400**: A restricted farm loss expires after 20 tax years.
- **500**: A listed personal property loss expires after seven tax years.
- **[a6_2]**: A non-capital loss expires after 20 tax years and an allowable business investment loss becomes a net capital loss after 10 tax years.
- **608**: Clean economy tax credit is defined in subsection 127.47(1).
- **[a7_2]**: Total(enter this amount on line 222 of Schedule 1)
- **638**: Clean economy tax credit is defined in subsection 127.47(1).
- **[a7_4]**: Total(enter this amount on line 335 of the T2 return)
- **amount 8A [a8_1]**: Enter amount 8A on line 128 in Part 2J of Schedule 130, Excessive Interest and Financing Expenses Limitation.
- **730**: The amount deducted must not exceed amount B in Part 2J of Schedule 130.
Enter the amount from line 730 on line 336 of the T2 return.
- **[a6_1]**: Enter each loss by year of origin, starting with the current year and going down to the 20th previous year.
