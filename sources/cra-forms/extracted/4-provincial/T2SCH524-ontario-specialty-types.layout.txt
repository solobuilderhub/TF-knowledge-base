· Use this schedule to identify the specialty type of a corporation carrying on business in the province of Ontario through a permanent establishment if:

    ­ its tax year includes January 1, 2009;
    ­ the tax year is the first year after incorporation or an amalgamation; or
    ­ there is a change to the specialty type.
