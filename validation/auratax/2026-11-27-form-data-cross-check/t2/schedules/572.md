# Sch 572: Ontario Made Manufacturing Investment Tax Credit

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/572.json` and `extras/572.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• If you are a qualifying Canadian-controlled private corporation (CCPC) that invested in capital property for which the expenditure was made and that became available for use after March 22, 2023, use this schedule to claim a refundable tax credit under section 97.2 of the Taxation Act, 2007 (Ontario). The credit rate is 10% and applies to eligible expenditures of up to $20 million in the tax year. For capital property that becomes available for use after May 14, 2025, the credit rate is increased to 15%.

• If you are a qualifying corporation that is not a Canadian-controlled private corporation (non-CCPC) and you invested in capital property for which the expenditure was made after May 14, 2025, use this schedule to claim a non-refundable tax credit under section 53.3 of the Taxation Act, 2007 (Ontario). The property must become available for use after May 14, 2025.

• Both the refundable and non-refundable credits will expire for expenditures incurred after 2029.

• The criteria to be a qualifying corporation are set out in subsection 97.2(3) or 53.3(6) of the Taxation Act, 2007 (Ontario). A qualifying corporation is, under subsection 97.2(3), a CCPC and under subsection 53.3(6), a corporation that is not a CCPC (non-CCPC), throughout the tax year. They carry on business in Ontario in the tax year through a permanent establishment in Ontario. For this credit, a permanent establishment is an office, a factory, a workshop, or a place in Ontario where the corporation uses substantial machinery or equipment. The corporation is not exempt from tax under Part III of the Taxation Act, 2007, for the tax year. Predecessor corporations in an amalgamation must have been qualifying corporations at the time the expenditures were incurred for their expenditures to qualify for the credit. A credit cannot be claimed for property for which the corporation or an associated corporation had previously made a claim.

• A qualifying corporation that is associated with one or more other qualifying corporations for a particular tax year must enter into an agreement with all those corporations to designate the amount under subsection 97.2(7) for CCPCs or 53.3(10) for non-CCPCs for each tax year ending in that calendar year. Use Part 1 of this schedule to allocate the designated amount. An associated CCPC or non-CCPC that has more than one tax year ending in a calendar year should refer to subsection 97.2(9) or 53.3(12) respectively.

• Eligible property is certain capital property included at the end of the tax year in capital cost allowance (CCA) class 1 or class 53 (class 43(a) after 2025).

• Expenditures for class 1 include costs for constructing, renovating or acquiring a qualifying building, or part of a building, located in Ontario and that became available for use in the tax year and after March 22, 2023, for CCPCs and after May 14, 2025, for non-CCPCs. At least 90% of the floor space of the building must be used at the end of the tax year to manufacture or process goods for sale or lease. Paragraph 1100(1)(a.1) of the federal Income Tax Regulations must apply to the building or part of a building because of an election under subsection 1101(5b.1).

• Expenditures for class 53 are for machinery and equipment acquired after March 22, 2023, for CCPCs and after May 14, 2025, for non-CCPCs, and that became available for use in the tax year and before 2026. Expenditures for class 43(a) are for machinery and equipment that are acquired and that become available for use in the tax year and after 2025. The machinery and equipment must be acquired for use in Ontario primarily to manufacture or process goods. The property cannot be excluded property, such as any property acquired under a contract with a person or partnership the corporation does not deal at arm's length with at the time the property was acquired. See subsections 53.3(22) and 97.2(17) for other excluded property.

• For both CCPCs and non-CCPCs, the expenditure for the purchase of manufacturing property in classes 53 or 43(a) could have been made either in the current tax year or in the preceding tax year. The expenditure for the purchase of buildings or part of a building in class 1 could have been made in the current year or in any previous tax year.

• Property has to become available for use by the corporation, as determined under subsection 13(26) of the federal Income Tax Act.

• To claim the credit, file this schedule with your T2 Corporation Income Tax Return. When claiming eligible class 1 property, also file an election under 1101(5b.1) with your return.

## Help text on individual lines

- **120**: The amount on line 120 is "0" if the total of line 110 is more than $20 million or you are associated in the tax year with one or more other corporations and did not file an agreement as required under subsection 97.2(7) for CCPCs or 53.3(10) for non-CCPCs of the Taxation Act (2007).
- **103**: The acquisition date is the date that the property became available for use. The available-for-use date must be after March 22, 2023, and within the current tax year.
- **amount 2B [a2_lB]**: If the corporation is not associated, enter $20 million. If the corporation is associated, enter amount from line 120.
- **308**: Include the address for CCA class 1 property
- **303**: The acquisition date is the date that the property became available for use. The available-for-use date must be after May 14, 2025, and within the current tax year.
- **amount 3B [a3_3B]**: If the corporation is not associated, enter $20 million. If the corporation is associated, enter amount from line 120.
- **330**: Enter the amount from line 330 on line 440 below.
- **amount 5A [a5_credit_tt]**: Equals line 490 in Part 4
