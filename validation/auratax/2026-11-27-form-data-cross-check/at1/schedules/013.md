## Help text on individual lines

- **125**: The immediate expensing limit allocated to the corporation, if it is associated with one or more eligible persons or partnerships (EPOPs); leave it blank otherwise. Enter it: the form refers to line 125 of federal Schedule 8, which the current federal Schedule 8 no longer has.
- **001**: If a class number has not been provided in Schedule II of the Income Tax Regulations for a particular class of property, use the subsection provided in Regulation 1101
- **039**: A DIEP reported in column 4 is a property acquired after April 18, 2021 by a corporation that was a Canadian-controlled private corporation (CCPC) throughout the year, which became available for use in the tax year (before 2024) and was designated as such on or before the day that is 12 months after the filing-due date for the tax year to which the designation relates. It includes all capital property subject to the CCA rules other than property included in Classes 1 to 6, 14.1, 17, 47, 49 and 51. A property can only qualify as DIEP in the year in which it becomes available for use. See subsection 1104(3.1) of the federal regulations for more information.
- **043**: The only amounts incurred before April 19, 2021 to be included in this column are certain inventory purchases from arm’s length persons or partnerships where the conditions in paragraphs 1100(0.3) (a) to (c) of the federal regulations are met.
- **045**: Immediate expensing applies to DIEP included in column 11.
- **[a1_T1_C10]**: If negative, enter "0"
- **[a1_T1_C11]**: If negative, enter "0"
- **035**: The program multiplies column 17 by the relevant factor of the class for property that became available for use in the calendar year of the tax year end (the federal Schedule 8 factors, which apply for Alberta purposes). If the relevant factor differs (for example, a tax year that does not follow the calendar year, under subsection 1100(2.01) of the Regulations, or property that became available for use in an earlier period), use the manual override and enter the adjustment.
- **037**: If negative, enter "0"
- **013**: Enter a rate only if you are using the declining balance method. For any other method (for example, the straight-line method, where calculations are always based on the cost of acquisitions), enter N/A. Then enter the amount you are claiming in column 23.
- **019**: The program claims the maximum CCA of the class. To claim a lower amount, or the amount of a class whose rate is N/A (a method other than the declining balance), use the manual override and enter the amount; it cannot exceed the maximum. If the tax year is shorter than 365 days, the declining-balance CCA is prorated by the number of days in the tax year / 365.
