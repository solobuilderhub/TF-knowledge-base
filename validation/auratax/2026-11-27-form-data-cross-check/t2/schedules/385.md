# Sch 385: Manitoba Odour-Control Tax Credit

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/385.json` and `extras/385.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• You can claim a Manitoba odour-control tax credit under section 10.2 of the Income Tax Act (Manitoba) if the corporation:
– has a permanent establishment in Manitoba
– has an unused credit from a previous tax year

• An eligible expenditure is the capital cost of a depreciable capital property acquired under the following conditions:– the corporation acquired the property after April 19, 2004, but before April 12, 2017, for the purpose of preventing, eliminating or significantly reducing odours from organic waste used or created in the course of carrying on its business in Manitoba
– the property became available for use by the corporation during the taxation year and before April 12, 2017
– the property has not been used or acquired for any other purpose by anyone prior to its acquisition by the corporation
– the property is eligible equipment (straw cannon, cover or sealing material for a wastewater stabilization basin, biofiltration equipment, storage tank or container for organic waste, spraying equipment for aerobic or anaerobic treatment of organic waste, soil injector for a manure spreader) or is declared eligible by the Minister
– the property is used in a process involving aerobic or anaerobic treatment, composting, drying or dehydration, or fermentation of organic waste

Note: The elimination of this credit does not affect the carryover of unused credits for eligible expenses incurred before April 12, 2017

• Use this schedule to:
– calculate the available credit and carry it forward to the following year
– request a credit carry-forward to a previous year
– transfer a credit following the amalgamation or liquidation of a subsidiary, as set out in subsections 87(1) and 88(1) of the federal Income Tax Act

• The unused credit can be carried forward 10 taxation years and back 3 taxation years
 Attach this schedule to your T2 return
