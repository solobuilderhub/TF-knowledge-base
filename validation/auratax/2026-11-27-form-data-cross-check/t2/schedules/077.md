# Sch 077: Clean Electricity Investment Tax Credit

From AuraTax 2.3.33, `fixtures/t2/specs/077.json` and `extras/077.json`. Not a primary source; check every rule against the published form.

## AuraTax's help text for this schedule

• Use this schedule to calculate your clean electricity investment tax credit (ITC) if you are a qualifying corporation or a qualifying corporation that is a member of a partnership that acquired clean electricity property that became available for use after April 15, 2024.

• Any property that is part of a project on which construction started before March 28, 2023, is not eligible for the clean electricity ITC.

• File a separate schedule for each qualified natural gas energy system that is assigned a system code by Natural Resources Canada (NRCan).

• The clean electricity ITC is a specified percentage of the capital cost of the corporation's clean electricity property based on when it was acquired and became available for use: 15% after April 15, 2024, and before 2035; nil after 2034.

• If you do not elect to meet the labour requirements in Part 2, you can only claim the clean electricity ITC at the reduced tax credit rate, which equals the regular tax credit rate (specified percentage) minus 10 percentage points.

• File this schedule with your T2 return on or before the later of December 31, 2026, and one year after your filing due date for the tax year in which the property became available for use.

• For more information, go to canada.ca/clean-economy-credits.

## Help text on individual lines

- **105**: If the property is a refurbishment, note this in the description.
- **122**: Assign a number to each designated work site and keep a record in case the CRA asks to see it later. If there is more than one designated work site associated with the property and you elect to meet the labour requirements, you must meet them for each designated work site to claim the regular tax credit rate.
- **125**: Exclude any part of the capital cost of the clean electricity property that is unpaid on the 180th day after the end of the tax year in which the property became available for use. Include this unpaid amount in the capital cost at the time it is paid.
- **130**: Include government and non-government assistance that you received, that you are entitled to receive, or that you can reasonably expect to receive in respect of the property (see subsection 127.491(9)).
- **135**: Include government and non-government assistance that you repaid (or that you have not received and can no longer reasonably expect to receive) that was applied to reduce the capital cost of a property for a previous tax year.
- **145**: 15% for property that became available for use after April 15, 2024, and before 2035; nil after 2034. If you do not elect to meet the labour requirements in Part 2, the reduced tax credit rate is the specified percentage minus 10 percentage points.
- **155**: If the labour requirements do not apply to the property, leave column 1M blank (see subsection 127.46(15)).
- **165**: Enter the amount from line 165 on line 185 of Schedule 31, Investment Tax Credit – Corporations.
- **310**: For each covered worker at each designated work site, calculate the number of days they were paid below the prevailing wage requirements. Add these amounts together and enter the result on line 310.
- **315**: For any part of a tax year after December 31, 2023, adjust this amount for inflation using the consumer price index and prorate it accordingly (see subsection 127.46(8)). For more information, go to canada.ca/clean-economy-credits-employer-compliance.
- **[a3_subtitle3]**: The maximum percentage of apprentices to journeypersons is based on the applicable law or collective agreement (see paragraph 127.46(5)(b)).
- **330**: For any part of a tax year after December 31, 2023, adjust this amount for inflation using the consumer price index and prorate it accordingly (see subsection 127.46(8)).
- **340**: Enter the amount from line 340 on line 580 of the T2 return.
- **525**: If the property is disposed of to a person who deals at arm's length with the corporation, enter the proceeds of disposition. In any other case, enter the fair market value. The amount in this column cannot be greater than the amount in column 5I.
- **550**: Enter the amount from line 550 on Schedule 31, Investment Tax Credit – Corporations.
- **665**: Enter the amount from line 665 on Schedule 31, Investment Tax Credit – Corporations.
