| [a01_l1] | • You can use this schedule to claim a Manitoba data processing centre investment tax credit for operator under subsection 7.19(1) of the Income Tax Act (Manitoba). This refundable credit for eligible data processing centre corporations is equal to 4% of the cost of data processing buildings acquired by purchase or lease, or constructed and 7% of the cost of data processing centre property acquired (by purchase or lease) in the current tax year and before July 1, 2013.
